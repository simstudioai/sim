## Usage Instructions

Manage Oracle Fusion customer subscriptions through CRM REST 11.13.18.05. Author subscription products, coverage, asset associations, charges, and discounts. Inspect billing and validation results, and invoke documented lifecycle actions. Oracle enforces business-unit access, privileges, and lifecycle restrictions. Some actions run asynchronously: inspect their result and subsequently read the subscription to confirm completion. Product visibility can depend on Oracle status-filter profiles. Customer master maintenance, Receivables invoice writes, CPQ, OCI subscriptions, and ESS job management are not included.
