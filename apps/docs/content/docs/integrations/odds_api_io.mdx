[Odds-API.io](https://odds-api.io) provides pre-match and live sports odds from 500+ bookmakers across 37 sports, with events, results, closing lines and odds scanners behind one REST API.

With the Odds-API.io block, you can:

- **Find events**: List the events of a sport or league for a date range, search them by team or league name, or read one event with its status and scores.
- **Compare odds**: Fetch the current odds of one event, or up to 10 events at once, from up to 30 bookmakers, every market included.
- **Pull history**: List finished events with final scores and sweep the closing lines of whole leagues over a season.
- **Run the scanners**: Get value bets for a bookmaker, selections whose odds dropped the most since opening or over the last 12, 24 or 48 hours, and arbitrage opportunities across bookmakers.
- **Look up reference data**: List the supported sports, leagues and bookmakers to get the exact slugs and names the other operations filter by.

Copy the API key from your [Odds-API.io dashboard](https://odds-api.io/dashboard) and connect it as a saved Odds-API.io account in Sim. Which bookmakers a key can query depends on the plan; see the [API documentation](https://docs.odds-api.io) for endpoints, markets and limits.

In Sim, the Odds-API.io block lets your agents alert on line movements, build odds comparison sheets, archive closing lines for analysis and answer questions about upcoming fixtures.

## Use the block [#usage-instructions]

Pre-match and live sports odds from 500+ bookmakers across 37 sports. List sports, leagues and events, fetch odds for one or many events, pull historical results and closing lines, and run the value-bet, dropping-odds and arbitrage scanners.
