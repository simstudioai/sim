**People** in the organization sidebar lists everyone active in your organization. Search by name, email, title, department, or location, or filter by department. Select a person to see their profile, the managers above them, and the people who report to them.

Organization admins keep this information up to date by importing it from your HR system or editing a profile.

## Import people data

1. Export your people from your HR system as CSV or Excel.
2. Open **People** and select **Import**, then choose the file.
3. Review the preview, then select **Import**.

Each row is matched to a member by email. The file needs an `email` column and any of:

| Column | Also recognized as |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | `job title`, `position` |
| `department` | `dept` |
| `location` | `office`; or `city`, `state`, and `country`, which are combined |
| `start_date` | `hire date`. Use `2024-01-31` or `1/31/2024`. |
| `manager_email` | `manager`, `reports to`, `supervisor`. Use the manager's email. |

**Download template** in the import dialog gives a file with these columns.

- A column the file includes replaces that field for every listed person; a blank cell clears it. A column the file leaves out is not changed.
- Rows are skipped when the person isn't a member, the email or date is invalid, a title, department, or location is over 200 characters, the person is named as their own manager, or an earlier row already updated the same person. The preview lists the first 200 issues with their row numbers.
- A manager who isn't a member, or who would make a reporting loop, is left as it was, and the preview says so.
- Nothing changes until you confirm the preview. Import up to 10,000 rows at a time.

## Edit a profile

Organization admins can open a person and select **Edit** to change their title, department, location, start date, or manager.

When someone leaves your organization, their profile is removed, and anyone who reported to them no longer has a manager recorded.

Imports and edits are recorded in your organization's [audit logs](/platform/enterprise/audit-logs).
