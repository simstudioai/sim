Ask Sim a question when you want an explanation, a summary, or an answer drawn from your connected apps. Keep the conversation open as you check the sources and ask follow-ups.

Your organization must enable Search and make a source available to you. [Connect your account](/search/connect-your-account) if that source requires a personal connection.

## Ask your first question

1. Open **Home** in the organization sidebar. From a workspace, open the workspace menu and select **Back to organization** first.
2. If the conversation mode picker is shown, choose **Search** before sending your first message.
3. Ask a specific question and press **Enter**.

For example:

> What belongs in a product requirements document? Use the Confluence template and cite it. Keep the answer to three short bullets.

Include the project, topic, or timeframe that matters. If you know where the answer lives, name the app or document:

> Find our launch plan in Confluence. Summarize the scope and the launch date, with sources.

Search mode finds and reads information. Use **Build** in a new conversation when you want Sim to take actions or change resources.

## Check the sources

Select an inline citation to open the supporting document. To review the conversation's references together:

1. Select the book icon and source count beneath the answer.
2. Select **View all sources** to open the **Sources** panel beside the conversation.
3. Select a source title to open the original.

Check the source's date and context before relying on a detail. If documents disagree, ask Sim to compare them and identify the difference. If the answer is incomplete, narrow the question or name another source to check.

## Follow up in the same conversation

You don't need to repeat the original question. Ask for the next useful detail:

> Turn the template's sections into a short checklist of information I need to gather.

> What belongs in the out-of-scope section?

> Which parts are filled in, and which are still placeholders?

Review the cited material for each answer. A summary can miss context, and an unavailable source can leave a gap. Sim can only retrieve content allowed by your account and the organization's source settings.

## Start from a document

If you already found the right document in **Search**, open its **…** menu and select **Summarize**. Sim starts a conversation focused on that result. Continue with questions about its decisions, dates, or next steps.

Your conversations remain private to you. Copying a source link does not give someone else access to the original document.
