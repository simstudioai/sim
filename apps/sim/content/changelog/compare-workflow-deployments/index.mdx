Compare any two workflow deployments, or review your draft against the live version. See prompt edits, added steps, and changed connections together so you can check exactly what will change before deploying.

Open **Compare** in deployment history or **View changes** on your draft. [Learn more about deployments](https://docs.sim.ai/workflows/deployment).
