Compare two saved workflow deployments, or check your draft against the live version. Sim shows prompt and setting edits, added or removed steps, and changed connections in the same view.

Open **Compare** in deployment history or **View changes** on your draft. [Learn more about deployments](https://docs.sim.ai/workflows/deployment).

### Also in v0.9.10

- **Readable file diffs:** Markdown files render additions, removals, and word-level changes with long unchanged sections collapsed. [Open the file editor](https://docs.sim.ai/files/editor).
- **Remembered panel widths:** Chat restores your resource panel width when you reload, return from settings, or collapse and reopen the panel.
