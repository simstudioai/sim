Open a workflow's deployment history and choose **Compare** from a version's menu. Pick two versions to review their changes side by side. When a draft differs from the live workflow, **View changes** opens the same comparison before you redeploy.

- **Review the details.** Inspect prompt edits, changed settings, and added or removed steps.
- **Follow the connections.** See which paths were added, removed, or rewired.
- **Keep your draft intact.** The comparison is read-only; opening it does not change or deploy the workflow.

For example, review a new instruction in a support agent's prompt and the [Slack](/integrations/slack) step added to send its digest. Select a changed block to move between its visual highlight and field-level details.

Deployment comparison is included in **v0.9.10**. Read the [deployment guide](https://docs.sim.ai/workflows/deployment), or compare versions through the [API](https://docs.sim.ai/api-reference/workflows/compareWorkflowVersionsV2).
