Start with an image, then describe what happens next. In v0.9.48, Video Generator can use an uploaded image or a file from an earlier step as the starting frame for supported fal.ai models.

Choose a compatible model to reveal **Reference Image**. Duration, resolution, aspect ratio, and audio options follow that model’s capabilities. Models without start-frame support remain text-to-video only.

Use your own fal.ai key, or Sim’s hosted access with an eligible paid plan or prepaid credits. Generation is billed by usage. Send the result to another step or [save it to workspace Files](https://docs.sim.ai/files/generating#save-an-existing-file).

### More creative options

- **v0.9.43:** Image Generator and Video Generator expose each supported model’s settings, so you can choose the format and controls your task needs.
- **v0.9.47:** [Higgsfield](/integrations/higgsfield) joins both generators. Custom models accept a prompt only; custom fal.ai endpoints require your own key. [Higgsfield setup](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/higgsfield).
