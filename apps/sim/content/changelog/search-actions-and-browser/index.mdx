Find the information you need, then act on it in the same conversation. Search Assistant can draft an email or create a Jira issue using your connected account. Writes ask for review by default: check the destination and content, edit supported fields, then allow or cancel.

Introduced in v0.9.37, actions follow your account permissions and organization’s source restrictions. Set **Allow**, **Ask**, or **Block** for supported tools under Integrations. Your organization must enable Search and the relevant apps. [Review actions](https://docs.sim.ai/search/ask-questions#review-actions).

### More ways to find and share

- **v0.9.43 — Web citations:** When enabled, Assistant can fill gaps with public web results and cite them alongside your connected sources.
- **v0.9.43 — Team shortcuts:** Create or copy a short `sim/` link from a Search result, Assistant citation, or file’s sharing dialog when Links is enabled for your organization. [Share a team link](https://docs.sim.ai/search/sim-links).
- **v0.9.45 — Multiple accounts:** Search across up to five accounts per supported app without disconnecting one to use another; some providers still allow one. [Connect accounts](https://docs.sim.ai/search/connect-your-account).
