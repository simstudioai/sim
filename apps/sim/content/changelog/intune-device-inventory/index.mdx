Add [Microsoft Intune](/integrations/microsoft-intune) to a workflow and select **List Devices**. Filter for noncompliant devices to prepare an IT review, or inspect detected applications and compliance policy status. The screenshot shows configuration only; no devices were queried or changed.

Connect a Microsoft work or school account with the appropriate Intune role. Your tenant needs an active Intune license and administrator consent. Remote actions depend on the device platform and permissions; restart, lock, and retirement also require explicit confirmation.

Included in [v0.9.15](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/releases/tag/v0.9.15). The [Intune setup guide](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/intune) covers permissions, filters, and supported actions.
