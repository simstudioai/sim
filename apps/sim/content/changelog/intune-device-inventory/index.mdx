Your agents can now read managed devices, installed applications, and compliance status from [Microsoft Intune](/integrations/microsoft-intune). Filter for noncompliant devices to prepare an IT review or bring device inventory into an agent's response.

[Connect Microsoft Intune](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/intune) to get started. An active Intune license, appropriate account role, and administrator consent are required.
