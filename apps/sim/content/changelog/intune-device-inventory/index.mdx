Your agents can now use [Microsoft Intune](/integrations/microsoft-intune) to bring device inventory and compliance information into Sim. Give an IT agent the context it needs to prepare a review, answer a device question, or summarize software across managed devices.

The integration lets you:

- **Review devices:** read device owners, operating systems, and compliance states, with filters to focus on the devices that need attention.
- **Inspect software:** list detected applications and the devices where they were found.
- **Check policies:** read compliance policy details and the reported status of devices covered by a policy.

For example, filter for noncompliant devices and pass the results to an Agent block to prepare an IT review. Include the device and owner information in the summary so the team has context for follow-up.

[Connect Microsoft Intune](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/intune) with a Microsoft work or school account to get started. An active Intune license, appropriate account role, and administrator consent are required.
