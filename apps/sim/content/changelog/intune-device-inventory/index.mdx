Your agents can now read device inventory and compliance information from [Microsoft Intune](/integrations/microsoft-intune). Filter devices by compliance state, inspect owners and operating systems, list detected applications, and read compliance policies with their device status.

[Connect Microsoft Intune](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/intune) with a Microsoft work or school account. An active Intune license, appropriate account role, and administrator consent are required.
