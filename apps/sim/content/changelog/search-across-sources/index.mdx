Search brings closer matches to the top across your connected sources. Documents whose titles and previews match your query can surface ahead of weaker results from another app, so finding the right information depends less on knowing where it lives.

Introduced in v0.9.25, this applies to keyword searches sorted by relevance in Search and Sim's search tools. v0.9.26 also recognizes short terms such as “AI” and “VM” when ordering matches. [Connect your sources](https://docs.sim.ai/search); results respect your account's access and your organization's source settings.

### Also in v0.9.26

- **Sign out other devices** in Settings → General ends your other sessions while keeping your current session and connected apps; sign-out can take up to five minutes.

### Also in v0.9.27

- **[Microsoft Excel](/integrations/microsoft-excel):** Find worksheets and tables, manage rows, write formulas, and format or sort ranges in OneDrive for Business and SharePoint workbooks. A Microsoft business account is required; reconnect an existing account for SharePoint browsing. [Excel setup](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/microsoft_excel).
