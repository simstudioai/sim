Ask a question about the page in front of you without copying it into another tab. The [Sim extension for Chrome](https://docs.sim.ai/browser-extension), introduced in v0.9.37 and expanded in v0.9.44, brings page text or a selected passage into your conversation.

Add another page for context, dictate a question, and edit the draft before sending. You control which pages are attached. Chat requires a paid Sim plan or available prepaid credits.

With Links enabled for your organization, type a shared `go/` or `sim/` shortcut in Chrome’s address bar to open a team destination.

### Also in Sim Desktop

These improvements shipped in v0.9.39:

- **Finish passkey sign-in:** On macOS, continue a website’s sign-in in an installed Chrome-family browser, then bring its session back to Sim.
- **Familiar browser controls:** Use keyboard shortcuts to switch tabs and move back or forward, save or copy images from their menu, and watch fullscreen pages inside the browser panel.
- **Update when you’re ready:** Restart to install a downloaded update in one click when idle, with a warning if work could be interrupted; your app windows reopen afterward.

[Download Sim Desktop and see the setup guide](https://docs.sim.ai/desktop).
