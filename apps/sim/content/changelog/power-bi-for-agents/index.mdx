Your agents can now query [Power BI](/integrations/power-bi) from Sim and use the results in a briefing, report, or follow-up. Connect business data that already lives in Power BI to the prompts and integrations in your agent.

The integration supports three parts of that process:

- **Find the right data:** browse accessible workspaces, reports, and semantic models, the datasets behind your Power BI reports.
- **Query a model:** use DAX, Power BI’s query language, to return the rows your agent needs.
- **Manage refreshes:** request a model refresh and check its history to see the reported status.

For example, query revenue by region, pass the rows to an Agent block for a sales briefing, and send the summary to Slack.

[Connect Power BI](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/powerbi) with your organization’s Microsoft account. Queries require workspace access, Read and Build permissions, and the **Execute Queries** setting enabled in Power BI. The guide covers permissions for each action.
