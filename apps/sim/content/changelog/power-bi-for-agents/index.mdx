Your agents can now query [Power BI](/integrations/power-bi) data, browse reports and semantic models, and manage model refreshes. Use DAX, Power BI’s query language, to pull the rows your agent needs. Refresh history lets you check the status of a requested refresh.

[Connect Power BI](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/powerbi) with your organization’s Microsoft account. Queries require workspace access, Read and Build permissions, and the **Execute Queries** setting enabled in Power BI.

### Also in v0.9.12

- **You.com:** Search the web, read pages, get cited answers, and run research from the [You.com block](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/youcom).
- **Browse connected sources:** Explore [Lucid folders](https://docs.sim.ai/search/lucid) and your private, shared, favorite, or recently viewed [Notion pages](https://docs.sim.ai/search/notion) without starting with a search query.
