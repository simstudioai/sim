## TL;DR

Sim, the open-source AI workspace for building, deploying, and managing AI agents, is a strong Workato alternative for teams that want self-service cloud plans, self-hosting, and control over model providers. Workato remains stronger for large enterprises that prioritize mature integration platform as a service (iPaaS) capabilities and governance over cost transparency or open-source deployment. Sim publishes this guide and is one of the products covered.

This guide ranks six Workato competitors for a specific buyer: a team that needs agentic workflows but does not want a quote-based Workato Enterprise contract. The ranking considers AI-agent capabilities, deployment control, pricing transparency, integration options, developer flexibility, and enterprise governance. All pricing and product details are current as of October 2026.

## What are the best Workato alternatives and competitors in 2026?

Sim is a strong fit for teams building AI agents, while n8n, Zapier, Make, Pipedream, and Microsoft Power Automate each fit different automation requirements.

1. **Sim**: Best for AI agents, self-hosting, and multi-model workflows
2. **n8n**: Best for technical teams that want self-hosted general automation
3. **Zapier**: Best for straightforward SaaS app automation
4. **Make**: Best for visual, branching business-process automation
5. **Pipedream**: Best for developers adding integrations to their own app or agent (Workflows shuts down March 31, 2027)
6. **Microsoft Power Automate**: Best for Microsoft 365 and Power Platform environments

This is not a universal ranking of enterprise integration suites. Workato can remain the better choice when an organization needs its enterprise integration depth, governance, and vendor-led implementation model.

### Is Sim a good Workato alternative for AI agents?

Sim is a strong fit for teams that prioritize AI agents, self-hosting, model choice, and self-service cloud plans over a traditional enterprise iPaaS procurement process.

**Best for:** Technical and semi-technical teams building agents that reason, call tools, connect business systems, and involve people when necessary.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Sim publishes its cloud plans](https://www.sim.ai/pricing), and workspace bring-your-own-key connections are available on every Sim Cloud plan. Organization-level model keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise.

**Strengths:** Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents. Its visual builder supports multiple model providers, API and SaaS integrations, conditional logic, human-in-the-loop steps, and deployment as reusable tools or endpoints. As of October 2026, the [Sim integrations directory](https://www.sim.ai/integrations) lists 270+ integrations. Sim can also connect to Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM on [any self-hosted deployment](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting).

**Limitations:** Sim is designed around AI agents rather than the full scope of a mature enterprise iPaaS. Organizations that need Workato-specific governance, implementation services, or a deeply established enterprise integration program may prefer Workato. [Sim's core is Apache 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE), but code in `apps/sim/ee` is governed by the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE), which requires an active Enterprise subscription for production use.

### Is n8n a good Workato alternative?

n8n is a strong Workato alternative for technical teams that want self-hosting, code-level extensibility, and broad general-purpose workflow automation.

**Best for:** Developers and operations teams that want to run conventional automations and AI-enabled workflows in one extensible system.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [n8n publishes cloud pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/) based primarily on workflow executions and offers a self-hosted Community Edition.

**Strengths:** n8n's [product and hosting documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/hosting/) describes a node-based editor, code and API connectivity, self-hosting, and AI-oriented nodes. It fits teams that want more infrastructure control than a cloud-only automation product provides.

**Limitations:** n8n is source-available rather than OSI-approved open source. Its [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license) permits many internal and noncommercial uses but restricts some commercial hosting and redistribution scenarios. Teams should review the license rather than treating n8n as equivalent to Apache 2.0 software.

### Is Zapier a good Workato alternative?

Zapier is a good Workato alternative for teams that want familiar SaaS automation and fast setup without managing infrastructure.

**Best for:** Business users automating common triggers and actions across cloud applications.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Zapier publishes self-service plans](https://zapier.com/pricing) whose allowances are tied to tasks and plan features.

**Strengths:** [Zapier's product documentation](https://help.zapier.com/) describes a large cloud-app ecosystem and a trigger-and-action model with a comparatively low setup burden for standard business automation. It is often easier to adopt than an enterprise iPaaS when workflows are straightforward.

**Limitations:** [Zapier is a proprietary cloud service](https://help.zapier.com/) rather than a self-hosted platform. Task-based billing can also make high-volume or highly branching workflows less predictable than teams expect.

### Is Make a good Workato alternative?

Make is a good Workato alternative for teams that need visual control over branching automations but do not require Workato's enterprise integration program.

**Best for:** Operations teams designing multi-step scenarios with routers, filters, transformations, and visible data flow.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Make publishes self-service pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) based on credits and plan allowances.

**Strengths:** [Make's help center](https://help.make.com/) documents branching, scheduling, transformations, routers, filters, and SaaS connections in a detailed visual representation of scenario execution. It is well suited to deterministic business processes that benefit from visual debugging.

**Limitations:** [Make is a proprietary cloud platform](https://help.make.com/) without a full self-hosted edition. Its credit model requires teams to estimate how frequently modules execute, especially in loops and branching scenarios.

### Is Pipedream a good Workato alternative?

Pipedream is a Workato alternative only for developers who want to add integrations to their own app or AI agent, because its Workflows product is shutting down.

**Best for:** Engineering teams that want managed authentication, prebuilt tools, and MCP servers for APIs inside their own product or agent.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Pipedream publishes Connect pricing](https://pipedream.com/pricing): a free development tier and paid plans metered by external users and usage credits.

**Strengths:** [Pipedream Connect](https://pipedream.com/docs/connect) provides managed auth, prebuilt tools and triggers, MCP servers, and an API proxy through SDKs and APIs. It is useful when a developer team wants to embed integrations rather than run a separate automation product.

**Limitations:** [Pipedream Workflows is in maintenance mode and shuts down on March 31, 2027](https://pipedream.com/docs/workflows), and Pipedream no longer accepts Workflows signups or subscriptions. Connect is a developer toolkit, not a visual builder, so teams that need to build automations without writing code should consider Sim or n8n instead.

### Is Microsoft Power Automate a good Workato alternative?

Microsoft Power Automate is a good Workato alternative for organizations already standardized on Microsoft 365, Azure, Dynamics 365, and Power Platform.

**Best for:** Microsoft-centric organizations automating desktop tasks, approvals, cloud applications, and business processes.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Microsoft publishes Power Automate pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-automate/pricing) across user, bot, and process-oriented licensing options.

**Strengths:** [Power Automate documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/) covers cloud flows, desktop automation, approvals, and connections across Microsoft's productivity, identity, data, and business-application ecosystem. These capabilities can reduce procurement friction for existing Microsoft customers.

**Limitations:** [Power Automate is proprietary](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/), and the complete service is not a self-hosted platform even though desktop flows and gateways can run within customer environments. Licensing can also span several products and automation modes.

## What key facts should buyers know at a glance?

Sim, Workato, n8n, Zapier, Make, Pipedream, and Microsoft Power Automate differ most clearly in licensing, deployment, and billing units.

- **Sim:** [Sim's core uses the OSI-approved Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE), while `apps/sim/ee` uses the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE); Sim supports self-hosting, and its cloud costs combine plan allowances with model usage or BYOK.
- **Workato:** Workato is a proprietary, vendor-hosted enterprise iPaaS; its [self-service plans](https://docs.workato.com/en/pricing/self-service/self-service) include Workato Free and Workato Pro from $75 a month for 2,500 credits, while its [Enterprise editions](https://www.workato.com/pricing) do not display list prices as of October 2026.
- **n8n:** n8n uses the source-available [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license) rather than an OSI-approved open-source license; n8n supports self-hosting, and its [cloud plans](https://n8n.io/pricing/) are based primarily on workflow executions.
- **Zapier:** Zapier is a proprietary cloud service without a full self-hosted edition; [Zapier plans](https://zapier.com/pricing) primarily meter automation through tasks and plan allowances.
- **Make:** Make is a proprietary cloud service without a full self-hosted edition; [Make plans](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) meter scenario activity through credits and plan allowances.
- **Pipedream:** Pipedream is a commercial hosted service; its [Workflows product shuts down on March 31, 2027](https://pipedream.com/docs/workflows), and its [Connect plans](https://pipedream.com/pricing) meter external users and usage credits.
- **Microsoft Power Automate:** Microsoft Power Automate is proprietary and is not available as a fully self-hosted platform; [Microsoft licenses it](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-automate/pricing) through user, bot, process, and related capacity options.

## Which Workato alternative is best for your team?

Sim is a strong fit for AI-agent teams, but the right Workato alternative depends on whether the team's primary constraint is deployment control, ease of use, visual automation, developer flexibility, or ecosystem alignment.

Choose **Sim** when agents, model flexibility, self-hosting, and self-service pricing matter most. Sim is especially suitable when a workflow must reason with an LLM, call business tools, pause for human input, and deploy as an agent or reusable endpoint.

Choose **n8n** when a technical team wants [self-hosted general automation](https://docs.n8n.io/hosting/) and accepts a source-available license. n8n is a stronger fit than Sim when conventional integration workflows are at least as important as AI agents.

Choose **Zapier** when nontechnical users need to [connect common cloud applications](https://help.zapier.com/) quickly. Zapier is the most familiar option in this group for simple trigger-and-action automations.

Choose **Make** when operators need to inspect [complex branching logic visually](https://help.make.com/router). Make is well suited to deterministic processes with transformations, routers, filters, and scheduled execution.

Choose **Pipedream** when developers want to [add integrations to their own app or agent](https://pipedream.com/docs/connect) through Connect. Do not start new automations on Pipedream Workflows, which shuts down on March 31, 2027.

Choose **Microsoft Power Automate** when [Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Azure, and Power Platform](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/) already define the organization's technology environment.

## When should you keep Workato instead of switching?

Workato remains the better choice when enterprise integration depth, centralized governance, and an established iPaaS operating model matter more than self-service pricing, self-hosting, or open-source control.

Workato is designed for organizations treating integration as an enterprise program rather than a collection of team-level automations. Its value is strongest when architecture, security, and operations teams need one governed platform across many business systems and are comfortable with sales-led pricing and implementation.

As of October 2026, Workato publishes [self-service pricing](https://docs.workato.com/en/pricing/self-service/self-service) for Workato Pro, which starts at $75 a month for 2,500 credits with no rollover, but [its Enterprise editions](https://www.workato.com/pricing) do not have list prices. Enterprise buyers should request commercial terms based on their required edition, usage, connectors, environments, support, and governance needs rather than relying on third-party cost estimates.

Workato may be worth keeping when migration risk exceeds expected savings, existing recipes are business-critical, or the organization already has the people and controls needed to operate Workato effectively. Sim is a better candidate when the new requirement is specifically to build AI agents with model choice, self-service cloud plans, and a self-hosting path.
