## TL;DR

The right Glean alternative depends on the job. [Coveo](https://www.coveo.com/en/solutions/digital-workplace) fits search-led digital workplace experiences, [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/enterprise) fits Microsoft-centric organizations, [Guru](https://www.getguru.com/features/verification) fits knowledge maintenance, [Dust](https://dust.tt/home/product) fits configurable workplace agents, [n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/) fits technical workflow automation, and Sim fits teams that need governed agents to retrieve context and act across tools.

[Glean](https://www.glean.com/enterprise-search) remains a strong option for permissions-aware enterprise search. Sim is not a pure enterprise search engine. Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents that combine knowledge retrieval with tool use and multi-step execution.

All pricing and licensing references in this guide are current as of November 2026 and link to first-party vendor sources.

## What are the best Glean alternatives?

Sim, Coveo, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Guru, Dust, and n8n address distinct enterprise requirements rather than serving as interchangeable replacements.

1. **Sim:** Fits governed agents that search for context and act across systems.
2. **[Coveo](https://www.coveo.com/en/pricing/):** Fits enterprise search across digital workplace, service, commerce, and website experiences.
3. **[Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/copilot-apis-overview):** Fits organizations centered on Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Graph.
4. **[Guru](https://www.getguru.com/features/verification):** Fits teams maintaining and delivering verified internal knowledge in employees' existing tools.
5. **[Dust](https://dust.tt/home/product):** Fits configurable workplace agents that use company context.
6. **[n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/):** Fits technical teams building workflow automation around APIs, databases, and AI models.

[Glean is compelling](https://www.glean.com/enterprise-search) when permissions-aware search and a unified employee assistant are the primary requirements. Buyers should consider alternatives when deployment control, action depth, ecosystem alignment, knowledge-maintenance workflows, or automation flexibility matters more than turnkey enterprise search.

## How did we compare Glean alternatives?

This comparison uses practical buying questions rather than scores or rankings:

- Can the product retrieve useful context from systems employees already use?
- Does it preserve source-system permissions when returning search results?
- Can it take governed actions, or does it mainly answer questions?
- What deployment options does the vendor document?
- What identity, access, audit, and administrative controls are available?
- Is the pricing model public enough for buyers to estimate cost before procurement?

Enterprise search, knowledge management, workplace agents, and agent workspaces are different categories with overlapping use cases. A tool can be a credible Glean alternative for one buying need without matching Glean feature for feature.

## What key facts should enterprise buyers know at a glance?

Licensing, deployment, and billing differ materially across these products, so procurement teams should compare implementation scope as well as subscription terms.

- **Glean:** Its [Core Suite](https://docs.glean.com/glean-core-suite-pricing) uses per-user, per-month licensing, while [Enterprise Flex](https://docs.glean.com/glean-enterprise-flex-pricing) combines per-user licensing with pooled usage credits.
- **Sim:** [Sim's core is Apache 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) and can be self-hosted. Code in `apps/sim/ee` is covered by the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE), and production use of that enterprise code requires an active Sim Enterprise subscription.
- **Coveo:** Coveo's [pricing page](https://www.coveo.com/en/pricing/) describes packages for service, website, workplace, and commerce use cases and directs buyers to request pricing.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot:** Microsoft publishes [enterprise Copilot plans](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/enterprise), with separate options for [Copilot Studio](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/copilot-studio).
- **Guru:** Guru's [pricing page](https://www.getguru.com/pricing) says pricing is tailored to organizational scale, knowledge complexity, and AI maturity.
- **Dust:** Dust publishes [Business and Enterprise plan details](https://dust.tt/home/pricing), including credits, connectors, governance, and support differences.
- **n8n:** n8n's self-hosted Community Edition uses the [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq), while its [pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/) covers cloud and paid self-hosted plans.

## How much does Glean cost?

Glean does not publish a universal dollar list price in its public pricing documentation as of November 2026. Its [Core Suite documentation](https://docs.glean.com/glean-core-suite-pricing) describes per-user, per-month licensing, while [Enterprise Flex](https://docs.glean.com/glean-enterprise-flex-pricing) combines per-user seats with pooled credits for advanced AI usage.

Buyers should request a written quote that separates these cost drivers:

- Number of licensed or active users
- Included data sources and connectors
- Search, assistant, and agent capabilities
- Identity, governance, security, and support requirements
- Implementation and connector setup
- Contract length and minimum commitment
- Usage allowances and overage terms for the proposed package

A useful total-cost estimate covers both subscription fees and implementation work. Third-party estimates should not be treated as authoritative purchase terms.

## Is Sim a good Glean alternative?

Sim fits teams that need to build governed agents that retrieve context and then take action across APIs and business tools.

**Fit:** CIOs, Heads of AI, platform teams, and developers that want an extensible agent workspace rather than a preconfigured enterprise search product.

**Pricing and deployment:** [Sim publishes cloud plan details](https://www.sim.ai/pricing), and workspace BYOK keys work on any Sim Cloud plan. Organization-level model keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise, according to the [Sim cost and BYOK documentation](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs#bring-your-own-key-byok). [Self-hosted Sim](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) can use local model services such as Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM without requiring Sim Enterprise.

**Strengths:** Sim provides a visual workflow builder for combining models, retrieval, APIs, conditions, human input, and application actions. Sim Cloud reduces operational work, while self-hosting supports infrastructure control and local models. Enterprise code covers SSO, SCIM, access control, audit logs, data retention, session policies, and related controls.

**Limitations:** Sim is not a drop-in enterprise search engine. Teams must design data ingestion, retrieval, permissions, credentials, and action policies for each agent. Organizations seeking an immediately deployed company-wide search layer may prefer Glean or Coveo.

For adjacent comparisons, see [Glean vs Dust](https://www.sim.ai/library/glean-vs-dust-enterprise-ai) and [AI agents for internal knowledge search](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-ai-agents-internal-knowledge-search).

## Is Coveo a good Glean alternative?

Coveo fits organizations prioritizing enterprise search, relevance, and search-driven digital experiences.

**Fit:** Enterprises that need search across employee, customer-service, commerce, or website experiences and want relevance tooling around those experiences.

**Pricing:** As of November 2026, [Coveo's pricing page](https://www.coveo.com/en/pricing/) describes product-specific packaging and asks buyers to request pricing.

**Strengths:** Coveo specializes in secure, unified search and relevance across several enterprise use cases. Its [digital workplace product](https://www.coveo.com/en/solutions/digital-workplace) connects enterprise systems and surfaces knowledge in employees' flow of work.

**Limitations:** Coveo is oriented around search and digital experiences rather than a general-purpose agent workspace. Buyers whose main goal is arbitrary multi-step execution across APIs should compare its action model with Sim or n8n.

## Is Microsoft 365 Copilot a good Glean alternative?

Microsoft 365 Copilot fits organizations whose knowledge, identity, collaboration, and productivity estate centers on Microsoft 365.

**Fit:** Enterprises that primarily use Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, Word, Excel, and other Microsoft services.

**Pricing:** As of November 2026, Microsoft publishes [commercial enterprise Copilot licensing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/enterprise), while agent-building capacity has separate [Copilot Studio options](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/copilot-studio).

**Strengths:** Microsoft documents that its Copilot APIs use the same authentication and authorization model as other Microsoft Graph APIs, with organizational controls enforced through Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft 365. [Microsoft's security documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/copilot-apis-security-authentication) explains those permission boundaries.

**Limitations:** Microsoft 365 Copilot is most compelling inside the Microsoft ecosystem. Enterprises with heterogeneous data estates should assess external connectors, non-Microsoft actions, licensing dependencies, and implementation effort.

## Is Guru a good Glean alternative?

Guru fits teams that need governed knowledge creation, verification, and delivery inside employees' existing work surfaces.

**Fit:** Operations, enablement, support, and people teams that need employees to find trusted internal answers and keep knowledge current.

**Pricing:** As of November 2026, [Guru's pricing page](https://www.getguru.com/pricing) says the vendor scopes an engagement based on organizational scale, knowledge complexity, and AI maturity rather than listing standard per-user tiers.

**Strengths:** Guru combines knowledge discovery with workflows for capturing, organizing, and verifying internal information. Its [verification feature](https://www.getguru.com/features/verification) assigns verification status, timestamps, and responsible subject-matter experts to knowledge.

**Limitations:** Guru is not a general-purpose self-hosted agent workspace. Buyers needing complex API execution or custom multi-step agents should compare Guru with Sim or n8n.

## Is Dust a good Glean alternative?

Dust fits teams that want configurable workplace agents grounded in company context.

**Fit:** Enterprises creating role-specific agents for knowledge work without making enterprise search the only interface.

**Pricing:** As of November 2026, [Dust publishes a Business plan and custom Enterprise options](https://dust.tt/home/pricing).

**Strengths:** Dust says its agents can use company knowledge, tools, schedules, triggers, and multiple models. Its [product overview](https://dust.tt/home/product) emphasizes shared knowledge, tools, skills, and governed human-agent collaboration.

**Limitations:** Buyers should assess Dust's connector coverage, source permissions, action depth, administration, deployment options, and contract terms against their requirements.

## Is n8n a good Glean alternative?

n8n fits technical teams that primarily need workflow execution, but it is not a direct replacement for turnkey permissions-aware enterprise search.

**Fit:** Technical automation teams assembling API workflows, retrieval pipelines, model calls, and application actions.

**Pricing:** As of November 2026, [n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/) is based on monthly workflow executions and includes hosted and paid self-hosted plans. n8n also documents a free [self-hosted Community Edition](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n/community-edition-features).

**Strengths:** n8n provides integrations, workflow logic, AI nodes, and cloud or self-hosted deployment. Teams can construct knowledge-retrieval workflows by combining data sources, vector stores, models, and application nodes.

**Limitations:** Teams must build and operate the search, indexing, retrieval, and permission behavior that Glean packages as an enterprise product. n8n's [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq) is source-available rather than OSI-approved open source.

## Which Glean alternative should CIOs choose?

Choose according to the primary requirement:

- Choose **[Glean](https://www.glean.com/enterprise-search)** when company-wide, permissions-aware enterprise search is the main purchase.
- Choose **Sim** when agents need to do more than return an answer, teams want a visual builder for models, tools, logic, and human input, or self-hosting and local-model support matter.
- Choose **[Coveo](https://www.coveo.com/en/solutions/digital-workplace)** when search relevance and search-driven digital experiences are central requirements.
- Choose **[Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/copilot-apis-overview)** when Microsoft Graph and Microsoft 365 define the enterprise knowledge layer.
- Choose **[Guru](https://www.getguru.com/features/verification)** when trusted knowledge maintenance is the main problem.
- Choose **[Dust](https://dust.tt/home/product)** when configurable workplace agents are the desired interface.
- Choose **[n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/)** when a technical team wants to construct the surrounding automation itself.

A proof of concept should use representative data, permission-sensitive queries, stale content, realistic actions, approval paths, failures, and administrative review. Procurement should evaluate operational ownership and implementation effort alongside subscription pricing.

## What else should enterprise buyers know about Glean alternatives?

Glean alternatives should be evaluated as different product categories with overlapping use cases, not as feature-identical substitutes. Enterprise search products package indexing, retrieval, relevance, and permission handling, while agent workspaces and automation products give teams more responsibility for designing context, credentials, actions, approvals, and operational safeguards.

The final choice should follow the workload. Start with the employee question or business action the system must support, identify the source permissions and audit evidence required, and then test the smallest realistic deployment before signing a broad contract.
