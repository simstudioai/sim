Sim, the open-source AI workspace for building, deploying, and managing AI agents, is a strong fit for teams that want a visual agent builder, broad model choice, customer-managed self-hosting, and [Sim’s Apache 2.0 core](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE), with the [enterprise exception](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE). n8n is strongest for integration-heavy technical automation, while Gumloop, Relevance AI, Make, and Zapier Agents suit buyers with different usability, deployment, and ecosystem priorities. This guide is published by Sim, one of the products covered.

Lindy is a hosted AI assistant product designed to automate work across business applications. As of October 2026, [Lindy lists more than 1,000 integrations](https://www.lindy.ai/integrations), while [Lindy’s pricing page](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) lists $50 in free credits and a Team plan billed per active user from $29.99/month, though some visitors see $49.99. Buyers commonly compare alternatives when they need customer-managed infrastructure, an OSI-approved open-source core, different usage economics, deeper workflow control, or access to another automation ecosystem.

Sim, n8n, Gumloop, Relevance AI, Make, and Zapier Agents differ mainly in deployment model, license, and how usage is billed, as of October 2026.

No product here is the right answer for every team: Make or Zapier Agents may still be the better pick when access to a familiar application ecosystem matters more than self-hosting or model-level control.

## Which Lindy alternative is best for your team?

Sim is a strong fit for teams that want open-source, self-hostable agent workflows, while n8n, Gumloop, Relevance AI, Make, and Zapier Agents each fit a narrower buyer requirement.

### 1. Sim: open-source, self-hostable AI agent workflows

Sim is a strong fit for teams that want to build, deploy, and manage AI agents in the open-source AI workspace with a visual builder and customer-managed deployment options.

**Best for:** Sim fits technical and semi-technical teams that need flexible AI agents, model choice, self-hosting, and more control over agent logic and infrastructure.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Sim supports workspace BYOK on every Sim Cloud plan](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs#bring-your-own-key-byok), while organization-level keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise; hosted model usage is billed at the applicable Sim rate.

**Strengths:** Sim combines a visual workflow builder with API connectivity, multi-model support, [customer-managed self-hosting](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting), and [local-model support through Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting/environment-variables) on self-hosted deployments. [Sim’s core is licensed under Apache 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE), an OSI-approved license, with [enterprise code under a separate license](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE).

**Limitations:** Sim requires teams to design the workflow logic rather than relying only on a prepackaged assistant, and operating a self-hosted deployment adds infrastructure responsibility. Sim’s enterprise features in `apps/sim/ee`, including SSO, SCIM, access control, audit logs, and data-retention capabilities, use the [separate Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE) and require an active Enterprise subscription for production use.

### 2. n8n: best Lindy alternative for technical automation

n8n is the best Lindy alternative for technical teams that prioritize application connectivity, code-friendly workflow automation, and customer-managed self-hosting.

**Best for:** n8n is best for developers and operations teams building integration-heavy automations that combine conventional workflow steps with AI functionality.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [n8n’s hosted plans use workflow executions as a primary billing unit](https://n8n.io/pricing/), while customer-managed deployments have separate licensing and operational considerations.

**Strengths:** n8n offers a mature workflow model, [built-in and community nodes](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations), [code steps](https://docs.n8n.io/build/code-in-n8n), APIs, webhooks, and a [self-hosted deployment path](https://docs.n8n.io/choose-how-to-use-n8n). Its technical flexibility makes it a strong incumbent when the job extends beyond AI assistants into general business-process automation.

**Limitations:** n8n can require more technical configuration than assistant-first products such as Lindy. Its [Sustainable Use License is a fair-code license](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license), not an OSI-approved license, and restricts some commercial hosting scenarios.

### 3. Gumloop: best Lindy alternative for fast cloud-based AI automation

Gumloop is the best Lindy alternative for operations teams that want an approachable [AI agent platform that automates agents with triggers, schedules, and the API](https://docs.gumloop.com/index) in a managed cloud service.

**Best for:** Gumloop is best for growth, research, and operations teams that value rapid setup and packaged AI-oriented workflow components.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Gumloop publishes credit-based plans](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing), with usage determined by the work an agent or workflow performs.

**Strengths:** [Gumloop builds AI agents that use your tools and data and runs them from triggers, schedules, and the API](https://docs.gumloop.com/index). It can be easier to adopt than a developer-oriented orchestration product.

**Limitations:** Gumloop’s documented experience centers on its managed service, while Sim and n8n document full customer-managed deployments. [Credit consumption varies with model use, tools, and run length](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/credits), so costs can be sensitive to workflow design and execution volume.

### 3. Relevance AI: best Lindy alternative for managed AI workforces

Relevance AI is the best Lindy alternative for teams that want to [organize managed agents and tools into Workforces](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/core-concepts/workforces) around business roles.

**Best for:** Relevance AI is best for sales, support, operations, and go-to-market teams evaluating role-based agents or multi-agent systems in a managed environment.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Relevance AI publishes plan allowances in Actions and Vendor Credits](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/pricing), while enterprise governance requirements are handled through its Enterprise offering.

**Strengths:** [Relevance AI packages specialized agents into visual Workforces](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/core-concepts/workforces), with tools and routing for multi-step tasks. Its workforce framing can help non-engineering stakeholders connect agents to defined organizational jobs.

**Limitations:** [Relevance AI’s published plans](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/pricing) do not list a customer-operated self-hosted deployment. Teams should also model usage carefully because [each tool run counts as an Action](https://relevanceai.com/docs/admin/subscriptions/plans), while model use consumes Vendor Credits unless BYOK is used.

### 5. Make: best Lindy alternative for visual app automation

Make is the best Lindy alternative for teams that primarily need [visual automation across many SaaS applications](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) and want to add AI steps to those scenarios.

**Best for:** Make is best for business operations teams whose requirements center on application triggers, data mapping, branching, and repeatable cross-app processes.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Make uses credits to meter module actions and other platform activity](https://help.make.com/credits), with current plan allowances published on [Make’s official pricing page](https://www.make.com/en/pricing).

**Strengths:** Make offers a mature visual scenario builder and [3,000+ app integrations](https://www.make.com/en/pricing). It is particularly useful when predictable app-to-app automation matters more than autonomous agent behavior.

**Limitations:** Make’s core workflow service is managed, although its Enterprise [on-premise agent can connect scenarios to applications and databases on a local network](https://help.make.com/on-premise-agent). Complex scenarios can become difficult to inspect, and AI agents are not the product’s only design center.

### 6. Zapier Agents: best Lindy alternative for the Zapier ecosystem

Zapier Agents is the best Lindy alternative for teams that want [simple AI agents connected to applications](https://zapier.com/agents) already used through Zapier.

**Best for:** Zapier Agents is best for non-developers and small teams that prioritize familiar SaaS connectivity and a managed setup experience.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, [Zapier measures Agents usage in activities](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/26559132765325-How-is-Zapier-Agents-usage-measured), with limits determined by the selected plan.

**Strengths:** [Zapier Agents can work across 9,000 apps](https://zapier.com/agents) and presents an accessible route from instructions to tool-using agents. It is a natural shortlist candidate for organizations already standardized on Zapier.

**Limitations:** Zapier Agents is a managed service rather than a customer-managed deployment. Zapier is also [migrating standalone Agents into AI by Zapier](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/47402591569805-Migrating-from-Agents-to-AI-by-Zapier), so buyers should evaluate the current Zap-based experience. Teams needing infrastructure control, local models, or deeply customized orchestration may prefer Sim or n8n.

## When should you choose Sim instead of Lindy?

Sim is a strong fit instead of Lindy when customer-managed self-hosting, [Sim’s Apache 2.0 core](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) with its [enterprise exception](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE), local-model support, BYOK, or explicit control over multi-step agent logic is a primary requirement.

Choose Sim when your team needs to:

- Run Sim on [customer-managed infrastructure](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) rather than depend only on a hosted assistant service.
- Use [Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting/environment-variables) with a self-hosted Sim deployment.
- Inspect and control branching, tool calls, conditions, and human-review steps in a visual workflow.
- Use workspace BYOK on any Sim Cloud plan.
- Build on [Sim’s Apache 2.0 core](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) while accounting for the [separately licensed enterprise code](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE) in `apps/sim/ee`.

Choose Lindy when your team prefers a hosted, assistant-first experience and [Lindy’s packaged integrations](https://www.lindy.ai/integrations) and [credit model](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) fit the intended workload. Lindy remains a credible choice for buyers who value managed setup more than infrastructure or licensing control.
