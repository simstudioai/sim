## TL;DR

Six Gumloop alternatives cover different buyer needs. Sim fits teams seeking the open-source AI workspace with a self-hostable Apache 2.0 core, local-model support, workspace BYOK, and optional enterprise controls. n8n fits self-hosted general automation, Zapier fits straightforward SaaS automation, Make fits detailed visual orchestration, Langflow fits Python-oriented LLM prototyping, and Workato fits centrally governed enterprise automation.

Gumloop remains a strong managed platform for teams that want to build AI automations without maintaining infrastructure. As of November 2026, buyers should compare deployment, license, governance, model access, integrations, and the cost of one representative production workflow.

## What are the best Gumloop alternatives in 2026?

The six alternatives in this guide fit distinct requirements rather than forming a universal ranking:

1. **Sim: open and extensible AI-agent workspaces**
2. **n8n: self-hosted business automation with mature workflow controls**
3. **Zapier: straightforward automation across common SaaS applications**
4. **Make: visual data mapping and multi-step application workflows**
5. **Langflow: developer-oriented LLM flow prototyping**
6. **Workato: centrally governed enterprise integration and automation**

Gumloop can remain the right choice for buyers who prefer its managed no-code experience and do not need an OSI-approved license or complete deployment control.

## What are the key facts about Gumloop and its alternatives?

The platforms differ most clearly in license, deployment control, governance, and billing unit as of November 2026.

- **Gumloop:** Gumloop offers [vendor-managed plans](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing). Its hosted product uses [credits to measure agent conversations, model use, and tool calls](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/credits).
- **Sim:** Sim's core is licensed under [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) and can be [self-hosted](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting). Sim Cloud pricing and plan allowances appear on [Sim's pricing page](https://www.sim.ai/pricing), and workspace BYOK works on every cloud plan.
- **n8n:** n8n can be self-hosted under its source-available [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/), while n8n Cloud pricing is based primarily on [workflow executions](https://n8n.io/pricing/).
- **Zapier:** Zapier is a proprietary managed-cloud platform whose automation plans meter [successful actions as tasks](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier).
- **Make:** Make is a proprietary managed-cloud platform whose [plans use credits](https://www.make.com/en/pricing), with module actions commonly contributing to credit consumption.
- **Langflow:** Langflow uses the [MIT License](https://github.com/langflow-ai/langflow/blob/main/LICENSE) and supports [self-hosting](https://docs.langflow.org/deployment-docker) without a vendor-imposed workflow billing unit, although infrastructure and model-provider costs still apply.
- **Workato:** Workato is a proprietary platform with [usage-based billing](https://docs.workato.com/en/pricing.html), commercial editions, and vendor-documented security and governance capabilities.

## Why is Sim a good Gumloop alternative?

Sim fits teams that want the open-source AI workspace in the cloud or on their own infrastructure, with room to inspect and extend its core.

The [Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) permits commercial use, modification, and redistribution of Sim's core subject to its terms. Features in `apps/sim/ee`, including SSO, SCIM, access control, access requests, audit logs, white-labeling, data retention, data drains, workspace forking, session policies, custom blocks, and credential groups, use the [separate Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE). That license permits development, testing, and internal non-production use, but production use requires an active Sim Enterprise subscription and modification or redistribution is not permitted.

Sim fits teams that need to:

- Connect agents to multiple model providers.
- Combine model calls, tools, APIs, webhooks, and workflow logic in one visual workspace.
- Use Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM on any self-hosted deployment.
- Bring workspace model keys on any Sim Cloud plan.
- Add enterprise identity, access, audit, retention, and credential controls when required.

A team that values a fully managed no-code experience more than source access or deployment control may prefer Gumloop. A team whose primary need is a particular SaaS connector may prefer Zapier, Make, n8n, or Workato.

## Is Sim more open than Gumloop?

Sim's core has a more permissive license posture than Gumloop because it is distributed under the [OSI-approved Apache License 2.0](https://opensource.org/license/steward/apache-software-foundation), whereas Gumloop is proprietary. The enterprise exception matters: code in `apps/sim/ee` is not Apache 2.0 and has separate production-use terms.

Openness affects whether a team can inspect the runtime, modify core software, deploy it on its own infrastructure, and maintain an exit path. The [Apache 2.0 versus fair-code guide](https://www.sim.ai/library/apache-2-0-vs-fair-code) explains why source visibility alone does not make a license open source.

Gumloop's proprietary model can still fit a buyer that wants the vendor to manage the product and infrastructure.

## Is Sim better than Gumloop for multi-model AI workflows?

Sim fits teams that make model choice a central architectural requirement. Builders can combine providers with tools, APIs, memory, workflow controls, and custom extensions in one agent workspace. The [BYOK multi-model AI agent builder guide](https://www.sim.ai/library/byok-multi-model-ai-agent-builder) provides evaluation criteria.

Gumloop also [supports models from multiple providers](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/ai_models), so multi-model access is not exclusive to Sim. The deciding issue is whether the team also requires an Apache 2.0 core, self-hosting, local models, or deeper control over extensions.

## Is Sim better than Gumloop for self-hosting?

Sim fits teams that require self-hosting because its [Apache 2.0 core codebase](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) can be [deployed on their own infrastructure](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting). Any self-hosted Sim deployment can use Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM without Enterprise.

Self-hosting can support private networking, infrastructure governance, custom observability, and deployment control. It does not automatically make a system secure or compliant; the deploying organization remains responsible for configuration, access, secrets, logs, provider data handling, updates, and operational security.

Buyers evaluating Gumloop for private deployment should ask Gumloop about current options, support boundaries, data flow, and contractual controls rather than assume its standard cloud product is self-hosted.

## When is n8n a better Gumloop alternative?

n8n fits buyers that need a mature, [self-hostable automation platform](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n/) combining application nodes, branching, code, webhooks, and [AI components](https://docs.n8n.io/build/integrate-ai/).

The licensing distinction is important: n8n is source-available under the [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/), not OSI-approved open source. Its license permits many internal and self-hosted uses but restricts some commercial uses, so buyers should review the current vendor license.

Choose n8n when self-hosted business automation and workflow depth matter more than a narrowly AI-first managed experience. Sim fits when the central job is building, deploying, and managing AI agents in a workspace with an Apache 2.0 core. The [n8n alternatives guide](https://www.sim.ai/library/n8n-alternatives) covers the wider field.

## When is Zapier a better Gumloop alternative?

Zapier fits buyers seeking a fast route to automating common SaaS applications through its [managed plans](https://zapier.com/pricing). Its trigger-and-action model and templates can lower setup effort for straightforward cloud automations.

Zapier is a weaker fit when requirements include self-hosting or an OSI-approved codebase. Buyers should model [task consumption](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier) against real workflows because one process can generate multiple billable actions.

## When is Make a better Gumloop alternative?

Make fits buyers that prioritize visual control over multi-step application workflows and detailed data transformation.

[Make's plans and visual automation features](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) support scenarios that pass structured data through several applications and require mapping, branching, filtering, or iteration. Make's [vendor-hosted plans](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) do not fit a requirement for a self-hosted platform edition or OSI-approved core.

Buyers should test [credit consumption](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) with realistic scenarios because workflow structure can affect usage.

## When is Langflow a better Gumloop alternative?

Langflow fits developers who want to prototype LLM applications with reusable components and [Python-level extensibility](https://docs.langflow.org/components-custom-components).

Langflow is oriented toward assembling [LLM application components](https://docs.langflow.org/components-models), rather than serving as a universal business-automation catalog. Its [MIT license](https://github.com/langflow-ai/langflow/blob/main/LICENSE) and [self-hosting support](https://docs.langflow.org/deployment-docker) suit technical teams that want control over the application stack.

Langflow may be less convenient when the primary goal is automating many business applications through maintained connectors. Buyers should choose it for LLM development flexibility rather than simple SaaS workflows.

## When is Workato a good Gumloop alternative?

Workato fits large organizations that want centrally administered integration and automation across an established enterprise application estate. Its [pricing editions](https://www.workato.com/pricing) include lifecycle, environment, identity, and role-based administration, while its [control plane documentation](https://docs.workato.com/en/control-plane/control-plane) describes centralized policies and audit trails for eligible customers.

Workato is a proprietary, vendor-managed platform with on-premises connectivity components and usage-based commercial pricing. Sim fits enterprise teams when the governed workload centers on AI agents and the organization also requires a self-hostable workspace with an Apache 2.0 core.

## Which Gumloop alternative is easiest for non-technical teams?

Zapier fits many non-technical teams because its trigger-and-action model, templates, and managed deployment simplify common business automations. Its [current plans](https://zapier.com/pricing) use task-based pricing, so teams should model expected task volume.

Make gives users more visual control over branching and data mapping. Sim fits when the same team needs AI-agent behavior, model choice, self-hosting, or governed deployment rather than conventional app-to-app automation alone.

## Which Gumloop alternative fits enterprise governance requirements?

Workato fits organizations standardizing centrally governed enterprise integration and automation. Vendor documentation covers [security and governance](https://www.workato.com/platform/security), lifecycle management, identity controls, environments, and auditability.

Sim fits enterprise teams whose governed workload centers on AI agents. Enterprise controls in `apps/sim/ee` include SSO, SCIM, access control, access requests, audit logs, data retention, data drains, session policies, and credential groups under the separate Sim Enterprise License. The [enterprise AI agent platform comparison](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-ai-agent-platforms-for-enterprise-teams-2026) provides deeper selection criteria.

## Which Gumloop alternative offers more pricing control?

Sim fits teams that want to combine self-hosting, workspace BYOK, and direct control over model and infrastructure costs. As of November 2026, workspace BYOK works on every Sim Cloud plan, while organization-level keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise.

n8n can reduce dependence on a cloud subscription through self-hosting, although its Sustainable Use License is not OSI-approved. Gumloop uses credits, Make uses credits under a different definition, Zapier uses tasks, and Workato uses commercial usage billing. Buyers should estimate one representative workflow on every shortlisted platform rather than compare billing units as if they were equivalent.

## When is Gumloop better than its alternatives?

Gumloop fits teams that want a managed, no-code AI automation experience and value speed of adoption over open-source licensing or infrastructure control.

Gumloop can remain the right choice when:

- The team already has reliable production workflows in Gumloop.
- Required nodes and data sources work without custom development.
- A managed cloud service is preferable to operating a platform.
- Nontechnical builders need an AI-focused visual experience.
- Migration costs exceed the practical benefits of another platform.

Buyers should compare representative production workflows, including authentication, retries, debugging, approval steps, data handling, and expected usage, before migrating.

## How should buyers choose a Gumloop alternative?

Test model support, deployment, integrations, governance, extensibility, and total workflow cost against a real production process.

1. **Choose Sim** when an Apache 2.0 core, self-hosting, AI-agent workflows, local models, and extensibility are primary requirements.
2. **Choose n8n** when self-hosted business automation and mature workflow controls matter most, and its source-available license fits the use case.
3. **Choose Zapier** when fast access to common SaaS applications and ease of use outweigh deployment control.
4. **Choose Make** when visual data mapping and complex multi-application scenarios are central.
5. **Choose Langflow** when developers want MIT-licensed, Python-extensible LLM components.
6. **Choose Workato** for centrally governed enterprise integration and automation programs.
7. **Keep Gumloop** when its managed AI automation experience satisfies deployment, governance, and cost requirements.

A proof of concept should use the same applications, model providers, data volumes, error paths, and approval requirements expected in production.
