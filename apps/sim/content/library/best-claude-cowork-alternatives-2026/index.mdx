## TL;DR

Sim is a strong fit for teams that need shared, deployed, and scheduled AI agents that several people build, run, and review, rather than tasks saved to one member's Claude account. Sim publishes this guide and is one of the five products compared. Gumloop is strongest for approachable cloud automation, n8n for technical automation teams, Zapier for broad SaaS automation, and Make for visual scenario building.

Claude Cowork remains a strong choice when an individual wants Claude to work with files and complete computer-based tasks. The alternatives below fit teams that need reusable workflows, shared credentials, centralized management, or agents that run as shared operational services.

## What are the best Claude Cowork alternatives?

The five alternatives below are ordered for shared, team-run agent work. Sim is a strong fit for that work because teams can build agents visually, conversationally, or with code, then deploy, schedule, and manage them in a common workspace while choosing models and connecting business tools.

1. **Sim:** a strong fit for shared, deployed, and scheduled AI agents across multiple model providers
2. **Gumloop:** best for approachable cloud-based AI automation
3. **n8n:** best for technical teams combining AI with application automation
4. **Zapier:** best for straightforward automation across a broad SaaS catalog
5. **Make:** best for visual, data-heavy automation scenarios

This ranking prioritizes the capabilities buyers usually seek when they outgrow an individual agent: team collaboration, unattended execution, scheduling, model choice, integrations, deployment options, and operational control. Buyers looking at the broader market should also read [Best AI Agent Platforms and Builders in 2026](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-ai-agent-platforms-2026).

### What key facts should buyers know about each Claude Cowork alternative?

Sim, Gumloop, n8n, Zapier, and Make differ most clearly in licensing, deployment, and the unit each vendor uses to meter cloud usage.

- **Sim:** [Sim's core is Apache 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE) and can be self-hosted, while `apps/sim/ee` is covered by the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE); [Sim Cloud measures billable usage in credits](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs) as of October 2026.
- **Gumloop:** Gumloop offers managed cloud software with [shared organization and team spaces](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/teams), and its [plans use credits](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing) as of October 2026.
- **n8n:** n8n can be [self-hosted on infrastructure a customer operates](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n) under its source-available Sustainable Use License, and [n8n Cloud pricing is based on workflow executions](https://n8n.io/pricing/) as of October 2026.
- **Zapier:** Zapier is a managed cloud service, and its [automation plans use tasks as a billing unit](https://zapier.com/pricing) as of October 2026.
- **Make:** Make is a managed cloud service, and [each module action in a scenario generally counts as one credit](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) as of October 2026.

## What is Claude Cowork, and when should a team choose an alternative?

Claude Cowork lets Claude take on multi-step tasks with access to files, connectors, and tools, while a Cowork alternative is usually better when a team needs centrally managed agents that run as shared services.

As of October 2026, [Anthropic lists Cowork on paid Claude plans](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork), including Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise, on Claude Desktop and, with some plan differences, on web and mobile. Cowork sessions and files are saved to the member's Claude account. [Anthropic's pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing/) lists Claude Pro at $20 per month when billed monthly in the United States and Max from $100 per month, and Enterprise at $20 per seat per month plus usage billed at API rates. Regional pricing, taxes, annual billing, and organization terms can change the amount paid.

Anthropic also documents [scheduled tasks for Cowork](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork). A user sets a task to run hourly, daily, weekly, on weekdays, or on demand. Scheduled tasks run remotely even when the user's computer is asleep, unless a task needs local files or apps, in which case it runs only on that computer. Buyers should still assess who owns each task, how permissions and credentials are shared, and how runs are reviewed against their need for centrally deployed team agents.

Choose Claude Cowork when one person wants Claude to work directly with files and applications in a task-oriented environment. Choose a shared agent workspace when multiple people must edit the same agent, manage credentials, trigger work from business systems, review runs, or operate recurring processes with shared ownership.

## Which Claude Cowork alternative is best for each type of buyer?

Sim is a strong fit for teams that want an agent to become shared operational infrastructure, while the other products fit narrower automation preferences.

### Is Sim a good Claude Cowork alternative for shared AI agents?

Sim is a strong Claude Cowork alternative for teams that need to build, deploy, schedule, and manage reusable agents in a shared workspace with a choice of model providers.

**Best for:** Teams moving from individual assistance to shared agents that connect models, tools, APIs, and approval steps.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, Sim offers cloud plans and self-hosting; current rates and included usage are listed on the [Sim pricing page](https://www.sim.ai/pricing). As documented in [Sim's billing and BYOK guidance](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs), workspace BYOK keys work on every Sim Cloud plan, while organization-level keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise.

**Strengths:** Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents. Teams build visually, conversationally, or with code, and workflows support multi-step agents, [scheduled execution](https://docs.sim.ai/workflows/triggers/schedule), event-driven execution, [human-in-the-loop approval](https://docs.sim.ai/workflows/blocks/human-in-the-loop), reusable connections to business tools, and multiple model providers. Teams can collaborate in one workspace rather than keeping an agent's operation with one person. [Any self-hosted Sim deployment](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) can use local models through Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM.

**Limitations:** Sim is not intended to reproduce Cowork's task environment exactly. Teams must design and deploy a workflow, configure credentials, and decide how runs should be triggered. Some enterprise capabilities in `apps/sim/ee` require an active Sim Enterprise subscription for production use.

### Is Gumloop a good Claude Cowork alternative for no-code AI automation?

Gumloop is a strong Claude Cowork alternative for teams that want approachable, cloud-based AI automations without managing self-hosted infrastructure.

**Best for:** Operations and go-to-market teams that favor a visual interface, templates, web data collection, and managed execution.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, Gumloop sells credit-based cloud plans; current prices, usage allowances, and team features are listed on the [Gumloop pricing page](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing).

**Strengths:** Gumloop combines visual workflow construction with managed AI steps and integrations. Its agents can [run automatically on schedules or external events](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/agent_triggers), making it relevant to buyers who want recurring work to run in a cloud service and be reusable by a team.

**Limitations:** [Gumloop is a managed service](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing). Credit consumption can vary with the components and AI operations used in a workflow.

For a dedicated comparison of this product category, see [Best Gumloop Alternatives in 2026](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-gumloop-alternatives-in-2026).

### Is n8n a good Claude Cowork alternative for technical teams?

n8n is a strong Claude Cowork alternative for technical teams that want self-hostable application automation with AI nodes, code steps, and detailed workflow control.

**Best for:** Developers and automation specialists connecting internal systems, SaaS applications, APIs, databases, and model providers.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, n8n offers cloud plans billed around workflow executions and a self-hosted edition; current rates and allowances are listed on the [n8n pricing page](https://n8n.io/pricing/).

**Strengths:** n8n provides a node-based automation environment, flexible triggers, [JavaScript and Python code steps](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.code), application connectivity, and self-hosted deployment. Its [AI tooling connects providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google](https://docs.n8n.io/build/integrate-ai). It fits organizations whose Cowork alternative must combine conventional automation with model calls and agent-like steps.

**Limitations:** n8n can require more technical implementation and operational work than a general-purpose AI assistant. Its [Sustainable Use License applies to the self-hosted community version](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq) and permits internal business use while restricting some commercial use cases.

Buyers comparing the wider competitive set can read [12 Best n8n Alternatives and Competitors for AI Agents in 2026](https://www.sim.ai/library/n8n-alternatives).

### Is Zapier a good Claude Cowork alternative for SaaS automation?

Zapier is a strong Claude Cowork alternative for teams that prioritize straightforward automation across a broad catalog of SaaS applications.

**Best for:** Business users who want managed triggers and actions for common sales, marketing, support, and operations tools.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, Zapier's automation plans use tasks as a principal billing unit, with prices and feature allowances varying by plan; current terms are listed on the [Zapier pricing page](https://zapier.com/pricing).

**Strengths:** Zapier connects SaaS applications through managed triggers and actions. It supports [scheduled Zap workflows](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496288648461-Schedule-Zap-workflows-to-run-at-specific-intervals), while Team and Enterprise accounts can [share workflows and app connections](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/40368119010701-Best-practices-for-sharing-collaborating-on-and-maintaining-workflows-in-Zapier).

**Limitations:** [Zapier is a managed cloud service](https://zapier.com/pricing) rather than a customer-operated deployment. Complex branching, high task volume, and AI-heavy processes require close attention to plan limits and task consumption.

For a broader alternatives analysis, see [15 Best Zapier Alternatives in 2026, Compared](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-zapier-alternatives).

### Is Make a good Claude Cowork alternative for visual scenarios?

Make is a strong Claude Cowork alternative for teams that want detailed visual scenarios with branching, data transformation, and scheduled SaaS automation.

**Best for:** Operations teams that need visual control over multi-application data flows and are comfortable configuring modules, mappings, routers, and error handling.

**Pricing:** As of October 2026, Make uses credits to meter module actions across its plans; current prices and allowances are listed on the [Make pricing page](https://www.make.com/en/pricing).

**Strengths:** Make provides a visual scenario builder with [scheduling](https://help.make.com/schedule-a-scenario), [webhooks](https://help.make.com/webhooks), and [routers and filters](https://help.make.com/router). It suits Cowork buyers whose real requirement is a recurring, inspectable process across cloud applications rather than delegated work in Claude.

**Limitations:** [Make is a managed cloud service](https://www.make.com/en/pricing). Its scenario model can become complex as branches and data mappings expand, and usage grows with the number of module actions a scenario performs.

## How should a team choose between Claude Cowork and an AI agent workspace?

Sim is a strong fit to replace Claude Cowork when the work must be owned and operated by a team, while Claude Cowork remains a good choice when the task centers on one person's context and files.

Choose based on the operating model rather than the most impressive isolated demo:

- **Choose Claude Cowork** when an individual needs Claude to work with files, connected tools, and computer-based tasks.
- **Choose Sim** when a team needs shared agents, flexible model choice, centralized credentials, scheduled or event-driven runs, and cloud or self-hosted deployment.
- **Choose Gumloop** when business users want managed, visual AI automation with minimal infrastructure work.
- **Choose n8n** when technical teams want self-hosting, code-level flexibility, and deep application automation.
- **Choose Zapier** when the priority is quickly connecting familiar SaaS tools through managed triggers and actions.
- **Choose Make** when the priority is visually mapping complex data movement and branching scenarios.

A useful evaluation should test one real process from end to end: who edits it, where credentials live, how it starts, whether it runs with shared ownership, how failures are reviewed, and how another teammate takes over. The best choice is the one whose operating model matches the work after the initial demo.
