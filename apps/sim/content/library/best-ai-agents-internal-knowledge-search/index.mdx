## TL;DR

Internal knowledge agents retrieve information from systems such as Google Drive, Confluence, Notion, Slack, and SharePoint, then use that context to answer employee questions.

- **Sim** fits teams that need a custom agent combining internal retrieval, live web research, business logic, approvals, and actions.
- **Glean** fits companies that want a managed enterprise search experience with source-aware permissions.
- **Dust** fits teams that want managed, role-specific assistants grounded in company knowledge.
- **Microsoft Copilot Studio** fits organizations centered on SharePoint, Microsoft 365, and Power Platform.
- **n8n** fits technical teams that want to assemble and operate retrieval pipelines from workflow nodes.

The right choice depends on whether the company wants a ready-made search product or a builder for creating a custom agent. All changing product facts below are current **as of October 2026** and linked to first-party vendor pages.

## What are the best AI agents for automating internal knowledge search?

These products address different deployment and operating needs rather than forming a universal ranking.

| Platform | Fits | Main tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Sim | A custom agent that combines internal retrieval, live web research, logic, and actions | The team must design source authorization into the workflow |
| Glean | Managed enterprise search across workplace systems | It provides less orchestration control than a general agent builder |
| Dust | Managed assistants grounded in shared company knowledge | It emphasizes a managed workspace rather than self-hosted orchestration |
| Microsoft Copilot Studio | SharePoint, Microsoft 365, and Power Platform environments | Its most direct advantages depend on the Microsoft ecosystem |
| n8n | Engineer-built retrieval and search pipelines | Permissions, retrieval quality, and citations require implementation work |

Compare source coverage, combined internal and web retrieval, permissions, answer controls, deployment, and extensibility. Answer quality also depends on document quality, chunking, retrieval settings, model choice, access controls, and evaluation data.

## Which tool combines web search and internal documents?

Sim fits teams that want to build one agent that deliberately combines private knowledge retrieval with live web search and additional workflow steps. A workflow can retrieve internal context, call a web-search provider, compare evidence, generate an answer, and route the result to another tool.

This approach is useful when an answer needs both company-specific and current public information, such as:

- Comparing an internal procurement policy with a supplier's current public terms.
- Answering a sales question with product documentation and current prospect news.
- Combining an HR policy with current government guidance.
- Summarizing internal competitive notes alongside recent announcements.
- Checking support procedures against a vendor's latest status or documentation.

Learn the retrieval pattern in [What Is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)?](https://www.sim.ai/library/what-is-retrieval-augmented-generation), then see a web-search implementation in [How to Give AI Agents Live Web Search with the You.com API in Sim](https://www.sim.ai/library/how-to-give-ai-agents-live-web-search-with-the-you-com-api-in-sim).

## When does Sim fit internal knowledge search?

Sim fits teams that want to build and control a custom knowledge agent rather than purchase a fixed enterprise-search experience. Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents. Its workflow builder can combine knowledge retrieval with model calls, web search, conditional routing, human input, and actions in connected systems.

That flexibility matters when an agent must do more than return a passage. An internal support agent might retrieve a policy, collect missing information, create a ticket, request human approval, and notify an employee in Slack.

A custom workflow does not make every connected source permission-safe automatically. The implementation should authenticate users, restrict retrieval to authorized indexes or credentials, filter results before generation, and log access. Organizations evaluating production enterprise features should review the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE).

[Self-hosted Sim](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) can use Ollama and endpoints compatible with vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM. Local models are a self-hosting capability and do not require Sim Enterprise.

## When does Glean fit internal knowledge search?

Glean fits companies that prioritize organization-wide search and source-aware access over custom orchestration. [Glean connectors](https://docs.glean.com/connectors/about) fetch content and permission maps from connected applications so search results follow source access controls.

Choose Glean when the requirement is primarily to deploy a managed company search experience. Choose Sim when the requirement is to build a customized agent that searches, follows business logic, and takes actions.

## When does Dust fit internal knowledge search?

Dust fits teams that want managed assistants grounded in company data without building every retrieval and conversation component from scratch. [Dust data sources](https://docs.dust.tt/docs/user-documentation/data-sources/overview) include synchronized workplace connections, tools, public websites, folders, and conversation files.

Dust is relevant when employees need multiple role-specific assistants over shared company knowledge. Sim fits when those assistants need deeply customized workflows, deployment patterns, or downstream actions.

## When does Microsoft Copilot Studio fit?

Microsoft Copilot Studio fits organizations whose knowledge, permissions, identity, and employee experience center on Microsoft 365 and SharePoint. Microsoft's [knowledge-source summary](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/knowledge-copilot-studio) documents sources including SharePoint, Dataverse, websites, and external systems.

This option may require more connector and governance design when authoritative information is spread across non-Microsoft systems. Sim offers vendor-neutral orchestration, while Glean focuses on managed cross-application search.

## When does n8n fit internal knowledge search?

n8n fits technical teams that want to assemble document ingestion, vector retrieval, web search, and answer delivery from workflow nodes. Engineers remain responsible for storage, embeddings, specialized databases that retrieve information by semantic similarity (vector databases), identity checks, citation behavior, and error handling.

n8n can be self-hosted, but its [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/) is source-available rather than OSI-approved open source.

## How should an internal knowledge agent handle permissions?

Authorization should happen before retrieval and generation rather than relying on the model to ignore restricted information.

1. **Authenticate the user:** Identify the employee before accepting a query.
2. **Authorize sources:** Determine which repositories, spaces, channels, folders, and records the employee may access.
3. **Filter retrieval:** Apply those permissions before passages enter the model context.
4. **Separate credentials:** Do not use one unrestricted service account for every employee query unless an authorization layer filters every result.
5. **Control citations:** Return source names or links only when the employee can open them.
6. **Record access:** Log the user, query, consulted sources, retrieved items, and resulting action.
7. **Evaluate boundaries:** Test with users who have different roles and deliberately restricted documents.

Source-aware permission synchronization is central to Glean's managed search model. Custom builders such as Sim and n8n provide more control, but the team building the agent remains responsible for designing and testing authorization.

## How do you improve answer quality?

Evaluate retrieval, evidence, and generation separately instead of treating every wrong response as a model problem.

- Index authoritative sources and remove obsolete or duplicated documents.
- Preserve metadata such as owner, update date, department, and access scope.
- Tune chunk size and overlap for the documents employees query.
- Retrieve multiple candidates and rerank them before generation.
- Require citations for factual answers grounded in company material.
- Let the agent state when available sources do not contain an answer.
- Separate internal facts from live web findings.
- Build evaluation sets from real employee questions and expected sources.
- Measure retrieval relevance, citation correctness, completeness, and permission leakage independently.
- Route high-risk HR, legal, security, and finance questions to a qualified person.

Observability becomes important after deployment. [What Is AI Agent Observability?](https://www.sim.ai/library/ai-agent-observability) explains how traces and evaluations expose failures across multi-step runs.

## What fits HR teams automating employee questions?

Sim fits HR teams that need a customized employee-question agent, while Glean or Microsoft Copilot Studio fit managed deployments when permissions and content already live in their respective ecosystems.

An HR knowledge agent can answer routine questions about leave, benefits, onboarding, expenses, and policies. It can also collect missing information, route sensitive cases to HR, create service tickets, and notify the employee when a person responds.

HR agents need strict controls. The agent should distinguish general policies from employee-specific records, prevent retrieval across unauthorized personnel files, cite the effective policy and revision date, and escalate ambiguous or legally sensitive questions.

A Sim workflow can add decisions and escalation around retrieval. Human in the Loop pauses a run and resumes it with submitted form fields. When the workflow should branch on approval, an approve-or-reject field must be checked by a downstream Condition.
