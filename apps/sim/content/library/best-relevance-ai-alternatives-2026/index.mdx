## TL;DR

Sim is the best Relevance AI alternative for teams that want flexible, multi-model AI agents without centering their deployment around packaged sales agents. Sim combines a visual workflow builder, broad model choice, bring-your-own-key support, and a self-hostable core, while alternatives such as LangGraph, n8n, Dify, Gumloop, and Zapier suit different technical requirements.

Cost depends on execution volume, model usage, infrastructure, and support requirements, so no platform is universally cheaper. The useful question is which Relevance AI alternative gives your team the right controls over those costs while supporting the agents you actually need to build.

## What are the best Relevance AI alternatives?

Sim is the best overall Relevance AI alternative for teams that need flexible AI agents spanning sales, operations, support, research, and internal processes.

The six leading alternatives are:

1. **Sim** — best overall for flexible visual agent workflows
2. **LangGraph** — best for developers building agents in code
3. **n8n** — best for connecting AI steps to conventional automation
4. **Dify** — best for self-hosted LLM applications and knowledge workflows
5. **Gumloop** — best for cloud-based visual AI automation
6. **Zapier** — best for straightforward SaaS automation with a large app ecosystem

Relevance AI remains a reasonable option for teams that want its [packaged AI workforce and sales-agent experience](https://relevanceai.com/pricing). The alternatives in this guide are stronger fits when the priority is broader workflow flexibility, self-hosting, code-level control, or a different billing model.

## Key facts at a glance

Sim provides an Apache 2.0-licensed core that can be self-hosted, while code in `apps/sim/ee` uses the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE); [Sim Cloud supports workspace BYOK on every plan and records hosted model usage at the applicable Sim rate](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs), as of October 2026.

[LangGraph provides an MIT-licensed, low-level agent orchestration library](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph/blob/main/LICENSE) that developers can run in their own environment, while [LangSmith observability and managed deployment are metered separately](https://www.langchain.com/pricing), as of October 2026.

[n8n uses its Sustainable Use License for the self-hosted community version](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq), which is source-available rather than OSI-approved, supports self-hosting, and [meters its cloud plans by workflow executions](https://n8n.io/pricing/), as of October 2026.

[Dify distributes its community software under a modified Apache 2.0 license with additional conditions](https://github.com/langgenius/dify/blob/main/LICENSE), supports self-hosted deployment, and [applies plan quotas to its managed cloud service](https://dify.ai/pricing), as of October 2026.

[Gumloop provides a visual workflow product](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/workbooks) whose [pricing system uses credits to meter automation activity](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/credits), as of October 2026.

Zapier is a cloud service that [meters successful automation actions as tasks](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier), as of October 2026.

Relevance AI is a cloud platform whose [published plans use Actions and Vendor Credits](https://relevanceai.com/docs/admin/subscriptions/plans), as of October 2026.

## How should you compare Relevance AI alternatives on cost?

Relevance AI alternatives should be compared by total workload cost rather than headline subscription price alone.

A platform with a low entry price can become expensive if it charges for every task, credit, model call, or workflow execution. A self-hosted platform can reduce vendor charges but introduces infrastructure, monitoring, security, and maintenance costs.

Use the same representative workload for every vendor:

- Monthly workflow or agent runs
- Average steps per run
- Expected model and token usage
- Number of builders and operators
- Required integrations
- Storage and retention requirements
- Development, staging, and production environments
- Human-approval steps
- Support and governance requirements

As of October 2026, consult each vendor's first-party pricing information before making a purchase decision: [Relevance AI pricing](https://relevanceai.com/pricing), [Sim pricing](https://www.sim.ai/pricing), [n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/), [Dify pricing](https://dify.ai/pricing), [LangSmith pricing](https://www.langchain.com/pricing), [Gumloop pricing](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing), and [Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing).

## 1. Sim: best overall Relevance AI alternative

Sim is the best overall Relevance AI alternative for teams that want to build agents for more than a predefined sales use case.

Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents. Its visual builder can combine model calls, tools, APIs, branching, data transformations, human input, and reusable workflows without forcing every use case into a packaged role.

### Why is Sim a good Relevance AI alternative?

Sim is a good Relevance AI alternative because it combines a visual building experience with control over models, credentials, deployment, and workflow structure.

Teams can use Sim for sales and CRM processes, but they can also build support triage, research, document processing, internal operations, approval, and data workflows. The [Best AI Agents for Sales and CRM Automation](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-ai-agents-sales-crm-automation) guide covers the sales-specific use cases in more detail.

[Workspace BYOK keys work on every Sim Cloud plan](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs). Organization-level keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise. Teams self-hosting Sim can also use [local models through Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) without an Enterprise requirement.

Sim's core is licensed under Apache 2.0. Enterprise features in `apps/sim/ee`, including SSO, SCIM, audit logs, access control, and data-retention controls, use the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE), under which production use requires an active Enterprise subscription.

### Who should choose Sim instead of Relevance AI?

Sim fits technical and semi-technical teams that want a visual agent builder without limiting themselves to packaged sales agents.

Choose Sim when you need:

- Multi-model workflows and BYOK
- Cloud or self-hosted deployment
- Local models on a self-hosted deployment
- Visual branching and multi-step agent logic
- Human input and approval patterns
- Agents spanning several departments
- An Apache 2.0-licensed core, with enterprise exceptions under the [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE)

## 2. LangGraph: best Relevance AI alternative for developers

LangGraph is the best Relevance AI alternative for software teams that want to define agent state, control flow, persistence, and tool execution in code.

[LangGraph is a low-level orchestration framework for long-running, stateful agents](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/overview) rather than a packaged sales-agent product. It gives developers detailed control over agent behavior and fits teams that expect to build a custom application around their agents.

### Why is LangGraph a good Relevance AI alternative?

LangGraph is a good Relevance AI alternative because it prioritizes programmable control instead of a predefined business-agent interface.

Its main advantage is architectural flexibility. Developers can define stateful graphs, introduce deterministic steps around model behavior, and integrate agent logic into an existing software stack. Its main disadvantage is the engineering effort required compared with a visual builder.

LangGraph's library is available under the [MIT License](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph/blob/main/LICENSE). Managed observability and deployment are offered through LangSmith, whose current commercial terms are listed on the [LangSmith pricing page](https://www.langchain.com/pricing), as of October 2026.

### Who should choose LangGraph instead of Relevance AI?

LangGraph fits engineering teams that treat agents as application infrastructure and want code-level control.

Choose LangGraph when developers will own implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance. Teams seeking a visual alternative should compare Sim, n8n, Dify, or Gumloop first.

## 3. n8n: best Relevance AI alternative for app automation

n8n is the best Relevance AI alternative for teams that want to combine AI steps with conventional triggers, integrations, and data movement.

n8n is especially useful when the workflow begins or ends in business applications such as a CRM, database, spreadsheet, messaging service, or ticketing system. Its [cloud plans include integrations and AI nodes](https://n8n.io/pricing/) while retaining the structure of a general automation product.

### Why is n8n a good Relevance AI alternative?

n8n is a good Relevance AI alternative because it supports broad app automation rather than centering the builder on packaged sales agents.

Teams can use n8n Cloud or [operate its community version as a self-hosted deployment](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq). That deployment choice can make costs easier to control for teams prepared to manage their own infrastructure.

n8n uses the Sustainable Use License, which is source-available but not OSI-approved open source. Its [license restrictions](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq) matter to organizations that plan to commercially host or provide n8n to third parties.

As of October 2026, [n8n Cloud pricing is based primarily on workflow executions rather than charging separately for every node in an execution](https://n8n.io/pricing/).

### Who should choose n8n instead of Relevance AI?

n8n fits automation teams that need AI inside integration-heavy business workflows.

Choose n8n when connecting applications is the central job and your team accepts its source-available licensing terms. The [10 Best n8n Alternatives for AI Agent Workflows in 2026](https://www.sim.ai/library/n8n-alternatives) compares n8n with other options in greater depth.

## 4. Dify: best Relevance AI alternative for LLM apps and knowledge retrieval

Dify is the best Relevance AI alternative for teams focused on LLM applications, prompts, datasets, and assistants that find relevant information in documents before answering, a technique called retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

[Dify combines visual workflows with models, tools, logic, and knowledge retrieval](https://docs.dify.ai/en/self-host/use-dify/build/workflow-chatflow). That makes it a stronger fit than a packaged sales-agent platform when the main requirement is an internal knowledge assistant or an LLM feature embedded in a product.

### Why is Dify a good Relevance AI alternative?

Dify is a good Relevance AI alternative because it supports general LLM application development and self-hosted deployment.

Dify is not simply an automation tool, however. Teams should assess whether its application and knowledge-base model fits the job better than the broader process orchestration offered by Sim or n8n.

Dify's repository publishes a [modified Apache 2.0 license with additional conditions](https://github.com/langgenius/dify/blob/main/LICENSE). Its managed service has plan-specific quotas listed on the [Dify pricing page](https://dify.ai/pricing), as of October 2026.

### Who should choose Dify instead of Relevance AI?

Dify fits teams whose primary deliverable is an LLM application, knowledge-backed assistant, or knowledge workflow.

Choose Dify when dataset management and LLM application structure matter more than broad automation across departments. The [Best Dify Alternatives in 2026](https://www.sim.ai/library/dify-alternatives) provides a closer comparison of self-hosted options.

## 5. Gumloop: best cloud-based visual Relevance AI alternative

Gumloop is the best Relevance AI alternative for teams that value an accessible visual cloud builder for AI-powered automation.

[Gumloop supports drag-and-drop workflows that process data, use AI, and integrate with external services](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/workbooks). Its approachable visual experience can suit operations, growth, and research teams that do not want to begin with a code-first framework.

### Why is Gumloop a good Relevance AI alternative?

Gumloop is a good Relevance AI alternative because it offers flexible visual automation without presenting every workflow as a packaged sales agent.

Gumloop does not provide the same licensing or deployment control as Sim's core, LangGraph, n8n, or Dify. Its relative cost depends heavily on [how an agent or workflow consumes credits](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/credits).

As of October 2026, Gumloop's published plans meter usage with credits; current allocations and feature limits are available on the [Gumloop pricing page](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing).

### Who should choose Gumloop instead of Relevance AI?

Gumloop fits teams seeking a polished cloud experience and broad visual AI automation.

Choose Gumloop when ease of use matters more than self-hosting or open-source licensing.

## 6. Zapier: best Relevance AI alternative for simple SaaS automation

Zapier is the best Relevance AI alternative for teams whose priority is straightforward automation across common SaaS applications.

Zapier provides a familiar trigger-and-action model and [an extensive integration ecosystem](https://zapier.com/developer-platform). It is less focused on custom agent architecture than Sim or LangGraph, but it can be the practical option when a team needs simple automations rather than deeply stateful agents.

### Why is Zapier a good Relevance AI alternative?

Zapier is a good Relevance AI alternative because it offers broad application connectivity and a low learning curve for common business processes.

Zapier is a cloud service without the same self-hosted deployment choice as several alternatives in this guide. Its task-based billing can be easy to understand, but multi-step or high-volume workflows require careful cost modeling.

As of October 2026, [successful actions generally count toward task usage under Zapier's published billing model](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier); current plan allowances are available on the [Zapier pricing page](https://zapier.com/pricing).

### Who should choose Zapier instead of Relevance AI?

Zapier fits teams that prioritize SaaS integrations and simple automation over model portability or self-hosted agent infrastructure.

Choose Zapier when the process is mostly deterministic and AI is only one component.
