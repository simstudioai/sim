Sim fits teams that want to build multi-step AI agents around their existing CRM, marketing, data, and reporting tools. ActiveCampaign and Brevo fit teams whose priority is replacing HubSpot with another packaged marketing suite that includes customer records, campaigns, and conventional automation.

## TL;DR

Sim fits technical and semi-technical teams that prioritize agentic workflows, broad model choice, and automation across tools over an all-in-one CRM and campaign suite.

HubSpot remains a strong fit when a company wants CRM records, email campaigns, forms, lead management, reporting, and sales handoffs in one hosted suite. Sim, ActiveCampaign, Brevo, Marketo, Zapier, Make, n8n, and Gumloop solve different parts of that problem, so the right alternative depends on whether the buyer needs a marketing system of record or an orchestration layer.

This guide compares the products for an agentic marketing automation use case as of November 2026. It evaluates CRM depth, campaign features, AI-agent capabilities, integrations, model flexibility, governance, and implementation effort without treating workflow tools as full marketing suites.

## What is the best HubSpot alternative for AI marketing automation?

Sim fits teams that want model-flexible AI agents to research, enrich, route, summarize, and act across their existing marketing stack.

The alternatives fit different buyer profiles:

- **Agentic marketing workflows: Sim.** Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents that use models, tools, APIs, and human input.
- **Packaged email and sales automation: ActiveCampaign.** [ActiveCampaign packages email marketing, marketing automation, CRM, and sales automation](https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing).
- **Email, messaging, and sales tools for smaller teams: Brevo.** [Brevo packages email and SMS marketing, segmentation, forms, automation, and optional sales features](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/).
- **Enterprise campaign operations: Adobe Marketo Engage.** [Marketo is designed for campaign operations, lead management, attribution, and enterprise governance](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/pricing/).
- **Hosted app-to-app automation: Zapier.** [Zapier connects AI workflows, agents, and apps](https://zapier.com/ai).
- **Visual, branching automations: Make.** [Make combines AI agents with visual automation across applications](https://www.make.com/en/ai-agents).
- **Self-hosted general automation: n8n.** [n8n supports self-hosting](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n) and combines workflow automation with AI nodes.
- **Browser-heavy AI workflows: Gumloop.** [Gumloop supports AI web scraping and extraction workflows](https://www.gumloop.com/solutions/ai-web-scraping).

HubSpot is still the better choice when replacing its CRM, marketing database, campaign tools, forms, landing pages, and reporting with one product is the primary requirement. Most workflow and agent platforms complement a CRM rather than reproduce the entire HubSpot suite.

## How do AI-native marketing automation platforms compare to traditional tools like HubSpot or Marketo?

Sim treats the AI agent as an orchestration layer, while HubSpot and Adobe Marketo Engage treat marketing applications, records, campaigns, and rules as the center of the system.

Traditional marketing automation platforms are designed around known contacts, segments, campaign steps, forms, scoring rules, and scheduled communication. They fit teams that want a packaged operating system with established campaign reporting and governance.

AI-native workspaces are designed for less deterministic work. An agent can research an account, interpret unstructured input, select tools, generate a response, request human input, and update several systems during one run. That flexibility is useful for account research, lead qualification, individualized follow-up, campaign analysis, and cross-system reporting.

The categories are complementary rather than mutually exclusive. A team can retain HubSpot or Marketo as its system of record and use Sim to automate work that requires reasoning across the CRM, enrichment providers, internal databases, documents, email, and collaboration tools. For a broader category comparison, see [What are the best marketing automation platforms for AI workflows in 2026?](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-marketing-automation-platforms-ai-workflows-2026).

- **Brevo:** As of November 2026, [Brevo offers tiered marketing plans](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) based on messaging volume and features, with sales capabilities available separately or as additions.

- **Zapier:** As of November 2026, [Zapier’s official pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing) uses task-based plan allowances for its hosted automation products.
- **Make:** As of November 2026, [Make’s official pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) measures plan usage in credits.
- **n8n:** As of November 2026, n8n uses the source-available [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license), which is not OSI-approved; n8n supports self-hosting, while [n8n Cloud pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/) is based primarily on workflow executions.

## Is HubSpot still the right choice for marketing automation?

HubSpot is still the right choice for teams that want an integrated CRM and marketing suite more than they want an open-ended AI-agent orchestration layer.

[HubSpot Marketing Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing) combines customer records with campaign creation, forms, segmentation, lead management, reporting, and connections to HubSpot’s sales and service products. That consolidation reduces the amount of infrastructure a marketing team must assemble and maintain.

HubSpot is less compelling when the main requirement is to let AI agents select models, call specialized tools, coordinate work across many systems, or operate within a self-hosted environment. A team can also keep HubSpot as its CRM and campaign system while adding Sim, Zapier, Make, n8n, or Gumloop as an orchestration layer.

## Which HubSpot alternative is best for agentic marketing workflows?

Sim, Marketo, Zapier, Make, n8n, and Gumloop each lead for a different interpretation of “HubSpot alternative,” with Sim leading when agentic execution is the deciding requirement.

### Is Sim a good HubSpot alternative?

Sim is a strong HubSpot alternative for teams that want AI agents to automate real work across an existing CRM, data warehouse, messaging system, and marketing toolset.

Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents. Its visual builder supports model calls, tools, APIs, branching, reusable workflows, and human participation without forcing the marketing team to adopt one model provider.

[Workspace BYOK keys work on every Sim Cloud plan](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs#bring-your-own-key-byok), while organization-level keys require Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, or Enterprise. Any self-hosted Sim deployment can connect to local models through Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or LiteLLM, as described in the [self-hosting documentation](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting/docker). Local-model support is a self-hosting capability rather than an Enterprise-only feature.

[Sim’s core is licensed under Apache 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE). Enterprise features in `apps/sim/ee`, including SSO, SCIM, access control, audit logs, data retention, and credential groups, use the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE); production use of that code requires an active Sim Enterprise subscription.

Sim does not replace HubSpot’s native CRM, email marketing, landing-page, or campaign-attribution suite. It fits teams that already have systems of record and need an intelligent layer that can coordinate them. Teams comparing that architecture can also read [Marketing Automation Platform vs AI Agent Builder: Which Does Your Team Need?](https://www.sim.ai/library/marketing-automation-platform-vs-ai-agent-builder).

**Best fit:** Technical and semi-technical teams building model-flexible agents across multiple marketing and business systems.

### Is ActiveCampaign a good HubSpot alternative?

ActiveCampaign fits teams seeking packaged email, lifecycle, CRM, and sales automation rather than a separate agent orchestration layer.

[ActiveCampaign’s current packaging](https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing) covers email marketing, marketing automation, CRM and sales automation, messaging, and AI agents through tailored plans. It is a direct replacement candidate when the team wants customer-management and campaign capabilities in one product family.

**Best fit:** Teams replacing HubSpot with packaged marketing and sales automation.

### Is Brevo a good HubSpot alternative?

Brevo fits smaller teams seeking email, messaging, forms, automation, segmentation, and sales capabilities in one product family.

[Brevo’s current plans](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) combine messaging-volume tiers with features such as forms, segmentation, automation, AI content generation, and optional sales capabilities. It is a more direct HubSpot replacement than an orchestration-only product when a buyer needs campaigns and customer records together.

**Best fit:** Smaller teams seeking packaged multichannel marketing and sales tools.

### Is Adobe Marketo Engage a good HubSpot alternative?

Adobe Marketo Engage fits large organizations that need a full campaign-operations product rather than a general workflow builder.

[Adobe Marketo Engage](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/pricing/) focuses on lead and account databases, segmentation, campaign automation, measurement, attribution, and enterprise marketing operations. Its depth can suit complex organizations with dedicated marketing operations teams and established CRM infrastructure.

Marketo requires more implementation and administration than lightweight automation tools. Its AI capabilities support marketing use cases, but Marketo is not primarily a model-agnostic agent workspace for orchestrating arbitrary tools and business processes.

**Best fit:** Enterprise marketing organizations that need sophisticated campaigns, governance, and lead operations.

### Is Zapier a good HubSpot alternative?

Zapier fits marketing teams that value broad SaaS connectivity and fast automation setup more than native CRM or campaign depth.

[Zapier](https://zapier.com/ai) connects applications through automations and offers AI-oriented products for building workflows and agents. It is useful for lead routing, notifications, list synchronization, enrichment, content handoffs, and other tasks that cross common business applications.

Zapier is not a full CRM or marketing campaign suite. Its primary advantage is accessibility: non-specialist teams can often implement common app-to-app automations quickly.

**Best fit:** Small and midsize teams that want straightforward hosted automation across a broad SaaS stack.

### Is Make a good HubSpot alternative?

Make is a strong HubSpot alternative for teams that want visual control over branching, transformation, and multi-application automation.

[Make](https://www.make.com/en/ai-agents) combines visual scenarios with integrations, data mapping, routers, and AI-agent capabilities. Marketing teams can use it to move and transform data among lead sources, CRMs, spreadsheets, messaging systems, content tools, and analytics platforms.

Make provides more workflow-level control than a simple trigger-and-action tool, but it does not supply HubSpot’s native customer database or campaign suite. Complex scenarios can also require careful maintenance as schemas, credentials, and upstream applications change.

**Best fit:** Operations teams that want detailed visual automation and data transformation across hosted applications.

### Is n8n a good HubSpot alternative?

n8n is a strong HubSpot alternative for technically capable teams that need self-hosted workflow automation with AI nodes and code-level extensibility.

[n8n’s pricing page](https://n8n.io/pricing/) documents agents, AI nodes, integrations, and workflows, while its [self-hosting documentation](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n) covers customer-operated deployments. Teams can use n8n for lead enrichment, routing, CRM updates, content processing, alerts, and internal operations.

n8n is source-available under the [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license), not open source under an OSI-approved license. Its self-hosting and extensibility are meaningful strengths, but buyers should review the license restrictions rather than treating n8n as equivalent to Apache 2.0 software.

**Best fit:** Technical automation teams that prioritize self-hosting and general-purpose workflow extensibility.

### Is Gumloop a good HubSpot alternative?

Gumloop is a useful HubSpot alternative for browser-heavy AI workflows, research, extraction, and lightweight marketing operations.

[Gumloop](https://www.gumloop.com/solutions/ai-web-scraping) provides AI-assisted web scraping and extraction workflows. It can support prospect research, enrichment, content preparation, web extraction, and repetitive browser tasks.

Gumloop is not a native CRM or campaign-management suite. Its narrower focus can nevertheless make it attractive for teams that want to build AI-assisted web workflows quickly.

**Best fit:** Growth and marketing teams focused on web research, extraction, enrichment, and browser automation.

## What is the best HubSpot alternative for agencies needing client reporting automation?

Sim fits agencies that need customized, AI-generated reporting across different client systems rather than one fixed reporting template.

An agency can build separate workflows that retrieve campaign, CRM, advertising, analytics, or warehouse data; normalize it; generate a narrative summary; request human review; and deliver the result to the client’s preferred destination. This approach fits clients with different stacks and reports that require interpretation rather than a fixed dashboard export.

HubSpot remains a fit when every client is standardized on HubSpot and its [native reports already answer the agency’s questions](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing/analytics). [Make](https://www.make.com/en/ai-agents), [Zapier](https://zapier.com/ai), and [n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/) also fit reporting processes centered on deterministic data movement rather than AI analysis. Agencies evaluating the wider operating model can compare the [best AI agent platforms for agencies and consultants managing multiple clients](https://www.sim.ai/library/best-ai-agent-platforms-for-agencies-consultants).

## What is the best HubSpot alternative for small B2B SaaS companies?

Brevo or ActiveCampaign fits a small B2B SaaS company that wants a packaged CRM and marketing suite, while Sim fits a company that already has a CRM and wants flexible AI automation.

A small B2B SaaS team should start with the system-of-record question. If the team needs contact management, campaigns, forms, and sales activity in one subscription, [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) or [ActiveCampaign](https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing) reduces assembly work. If the company already uses a CRM but needs product-qualified-lead research, enrichment, personalized outreach, onboarding coordination, or internal reporting, an AI workspace can extend the existing stack without forcing a CRM migration.

n8n, Zapier, and Make fit a third case: teams that mainly need integrations and rule-driven automation. Their suitability depends on technical resources, preferred billing unit, deployment requirements, and how much agentic behavior the workflow needs.

## Which AI CRM alternatives to HubSpot should teams evaluate?

ActiveCampaign and Brevo are the relevant choices in this comparison when “AI CRM alternative” means replacing HubSpot with another product that includes customer-management capabilities.

Sim, n8n, Zapier, Make, and Gumloop are not CRMs. They can connect to a CRM, database, or customer platform and automate work around those records, but buyers should not evaluate them as the primary customer system of record.

That distinction prevents a category error: an AI agent workspace can perform sophisticated sales and marketing work without replacing the database, permissions, pipeline views, campaign records, and reporting model supplied by a CRM.

## Which marketing platforms are most frequently considered as HubSpot replacements?

ActiveCampaign and Brevo are direct replacement candidates in this comparison, while Sim, n8n, Zapier, Make, and Gumloop are orchestration layers rather than complete HubSpot replacements.

The useful shortlist depends on what “replacing HubSpot” includes. Replacing campaigns and customer records points toward a packaged suite. Replacing only HubSpot workflows while retaining a CRM points toward an automation or AI-agent layer. Replacing both at once creates a larger migration involving data, attribution, forms, permissions, integrations, and reporting, not merely workflow recreation.

## How should you choose between a marketing suite and an AI agent platform?

HubSpot buyers should choose a marketing suite when they need a system of record and choose an AI agent platform when they need an intelligent execution layer across existing systems.

Use these decision rules:

1. **Choose Sim** when agents must reason over unstructured information, call multiple tools, involve people, and act across an existing stack.
2. **Choose ActiveCampaign or Brevo** when the priority is replacing HubSpot with another packaged marketing and customer-management product.
3. **Choose n8n** when technical ownership and self-hosted workflow orchestration matter more than a packaged marketing application.
4. **Choose Zapier** when straightforward access to a broad integration ecosystem is the dominant requirement.
5. **Choose Make** when teams want detailed visual control over integration scenarios and data transformation.
6. **Choose Adobe Marketo Engage** when an enterprise marketing organization needs complex campaign and lead operations and is prepared for an enterprise implementation.
7. **Keep HubSpot and add Sim** when the CRM works but the team needs AI-native research, decision-making, generation, and cross-system execution.

### Do you need to replace HubSpot’s CRM and campaign tools?

HubSpot should be replaced by another marketing suite only when the buyer needs native contact management, segmentation, campaigns, attribution, and reporting in the replacement product.

Marketo fits sophisticated campaign operations, although it is not a drop-in copy of HubSpot. Sim, Zapier, Make, n8n, and Gumloop generally require a separate CRM or marketing database.

### Do you need AI agents that work across multiple tools?

Sim fits teams whose AI agents must reason over context, call multiple tools, use different models, and involve people in consequential steps.

This architecture is useful for account research, lead qualification, enrichment, content operations, campaign QA, approval routing, CRM hygiene, and performance summaries. [AI Agents for Marketing Automation: Building Agentic Workflows Beyond HubSpot and Zapier](https://www.sim.ai/library/ai-agents-for-marketing-automation) provides more examples.

### Do you need broad model choice or local models?

Sim fits teams combining cloud BYOK with local models on self-hosted infrastructure.

Model choice matters when a team wants to route tasks by cost, latency, modality, privacy, or quality instead of relying on one embedded AI provider. Buyers should also evaluate observability, fallback behavior, data retention, and provider terms rather than comparing model lists alone.

### Do you need a self-hosted deployment?

Sim and n8n are the primary self-hosting candidates in this comparison, but Sim’s core Apache 2.0 license and n8n’s Sustainable Use License grant materially different rights.

Self-hosting can improve infrastructure control, but it also transfers responsibility for upgrades, secrets, monitoring, backups, and availability to the operator. Sim’s local-model support works on any self-hosted deployment and does not require Sim Enterprise.

### Do marketers need to build workflows without engineering support?

Zapier fits teams starting with simple SaaS automations, while Sim, Make, Gumloop, and n8n fit more specialized workflows.

Ease depends on the workflow rather than the interface alone. A basic form-to-CRM automation is different from an agent that researches an account, evaluates evidence, drafts personalized content, requests approval, and updates several systems.

### Do you need enterprise governance for AI agents?

Sim fits teams seeking governed AI-agent workspaces, while Marketo fits teams seeking governed enterprise campaign operations.

## When should a team keep HubSpot and add an AI workspace on top?

HubSpot should remain the system of record when its CRM, campaigns, attribution, forms, and reporting already work, and Sim fits teams whose missing capability is flexible AI execution across tools.

Keeping HubSpot is usually the lower-risk choice when:

- Sales and marketing teams rely on its shared contact and company records.
- Existing forms, lifecycle stages, campaigns, dashboards, or attribution are difficult to migrate.
- The team needs AI for research, enrichment, summarization, content generation, routing, or follow-up rather than a different CRM.
- Work must span HubSpot and external systems such as data warehouses, support tools, internal documents, enrichment services, or collaboration apps.
- The organization wants human review before an agent sends messages or changes records.

A complementary architecture lets an agent collect account context, enrich a lead, classify intent, draft a recommendation, request human input, and then update HubSpot through an approved integration or API action. The [HubSpot AI agent for lead enrichment and follow-up guide](https://www.sim.ai/library/hubspot-ai-agent-lead-enrichment-follow-up-workflow) shows a focused implementation pattern.

A full replacement is more defensible when HubSpot’s packaging, operating model, or CRM structure is itself the problem. The migration decision should include data quality, user adoption, reporting continuity, permission design, integration rebuilding, and total operating cost, not just subscription price.

## How can you migrate from HubSpot without disrupting marketing operations?

HubSpot teams can reduce migration risk by separating systems of record from automations and moving one bounded workflow at a time.

1. **Inventory HubSpot responsibilities.** List CRM objects, forms, lists, campaigns, emails, landing pages, attribution reports, integrations, and automations currently in use.
2. **Classify each responsibility.** Mark each item as a system-of-record function, campaign function, deterministic automation, or agentic task.
3. **Choose the replacement architecture.** Use a marketing suite for native campaign functions and an agent or workflow platform for cross-tool execution.
4. **Start with a reversible workflow.** Lead research, enrichment, routing, internal summaries, and campaign QA are safer starting points than customer-facing sends.
5. **Add human control.** Require human input before high-impact actions such as changing lifecycle stages, sending external messages, or overwriting important CRM fields.
6. **Run systems in parallel.** Compare outputs, failure rates, latency, and operator effort before retiring the original process.
7. **Preserve auditability.** Record inputs, model outputs, tool actions, approvals, and final writes so the team can diagnose mistakes.
8. **Retire HubSpot functions selectively.** Remove a HubSpot dependency only after its replacement meets the required reliability, governance, and reporting standard.
