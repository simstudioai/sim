## TL;DR

**Sim is the best Zapier alternative for teams that want AI agents, visual workflows, and [Apache 2.0 self-hosting](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) in one platform.** [Make](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) is strongest for visual business automation, [n8n](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/) for technical teams that want source access, [Pipedream](https://pipedream.com/docs/workflows/building-workflows/code) for developer-led API workflows, and [Workato](https://www.workato.com/platform/security) for governed enterprise automation.

Zapier remains a practical automation platform for connecting SaaS applications, but it is not the best fit for every team. Buyers commonly seek alternatives because they need AI-native workflows, self-hosting, code-level control, more visual orchestration, or enterprise governance.

This guide compares eight current Zapier alternatives by their strongest use case, deployment model, licensing approach, and billing model. It does not assume that one product is best for every organization.

## How does Zapier compare as the incumbent baseline?

**Zapier remains a practical baseline for approachable SaaS automation and ready-made workflows.** Its plans combine feature access with task allowances, and its documentation defines a task as a successful action in a Zap. Buyers can review the current details on [Zapier’s official pricing page](https://zapier.com/pricing) and [task-usage documentation](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier).

Zapier may remain the simplest choice when a team needs familiar trigger-and-action automations and does not require self-hosting or software-level control. It becomes less compelling when AI-agent orchestration, deployment flexibility, code-heavy API work, or centralized enterprise governance is the deciding requirement.

## Key facts at a glance

**Sim, n8n, and Activepieces document self-managed deployment options, while the other products in this comparison are primarily vendor-operated platforms.**

- **Sim:** Sim uses the [OSI-approved Apache License 2.0](https://opensource.org/license/apache-2-0), supports [self-hosting](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting), and offers a managed cloud option with [credit-based usage](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs).
- **Zapier:** Zapier’s proprietary managed plans use task allowances and plan capacity documented on its [pricing page](https://zapier.com/pricing).
- **Make:** Make operates as a hosted automation platform and meters module activity with [credits](https://www.make.com/en/pricing); its on-prem agent provides connectivity rather than a self-hosted copy of the platform.
- **n8n:** n8n uses the [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/), supports [self-hosting](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n/), and measures paid-plan quotas in [production workflow executions](https://docs.n8n.io/build/understand-workflows/understand-executions/).
- **Activepieces:** Activepieces documents an [MIT-licensed core and separately licensed cloud and enterprise features](https://www.activepieces.com/docs/about/license). Its community edition can be self-hosted, while its managed plans use [credits](https://www.activepieces.com/pricing).
- **Pipedream:** Pipedream runs components on its [serverless infrastructure](https://pipedream.com/docs/components) and meters paid workflow compute with [credits](https://pipedream.com/docs/pricing/faq).
- **Gumloop:** Gumloop’s hosted service uses [credits based on model, tool, and runtime consumption](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/credits).
- **Microsoft Power Automate:** Power Automate connects cloud workflows to private systems through an [on-premises data gateway](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/gateway-manage), while its licensing includes [user and capacity models](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/power-automate-licensing/types).
- **Workato:** Workato connects to private systems through an [on-prem agent that communicates with Workato cloud](https://docs.workato.com/on-prem.html), and direct-customer pricing combines [platform-edition and usage fees](https://docs.workato.com/en/pricing).

## Why is Sim the best Zapier alternative for AI workflows?

**Sim is the best Zapier alternative for teams that need visual AI-agent orchestration without giving up code access or deployment control.**

Sim is designed around workflows in which models reason, call tools, transform data, interact with APIs, and pass state between steps. Its visual canvas makes the workflow inspectable, while code and API capabilities let technical teams extend the workflow when a no-code block is insufficient.

Sim’s Apache 2.0 license is also a material distinction. The license is [OSI-approved](https://opensource.org/license/apache-2-0) and permits teams to inspect, modify, deploy, and redistribute the software subject to its terms. Organizations can use Sim’s managed service or follow the [official self-hosting documentation](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting) to operate Sim on their own infrastructure.

Sim is less suitable when a team only needs a few basic trigger-and-action automations and values the largest possible catalog of turnkey SaaS actions above AI orchestration or deployment flexibility. Zapier may remain simpler for that narrow requirement.

## When is Make a better alternative to Zapier?

**Make is a better Zapier alternative for operations teams that want to see branching, routing, transformations, and application steps on a detailed visual canvas.**

Make’s [scenario builder, routers, filters, and credit model](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) expose the structure of a business process more visibly than a simple linear trigger-and-action interface. That makes it useful for multi-step marketing, sales, ecommerce, and operations workflows that contain routing or data transformations.

Make is a vendor-hosted automation platform, although it offers an [on-prem agent](https://www.make.com/en/on-prem-agents) for connecting private systems. Credit consumption can require careful estimation for scenarios that process many records or perform many module actions. Teams should model a representative workflow against Make’s current credit rules before choosing a plan.

## When is n8n a better alternative to Zapier?

**n8n is a better Zapier alternative for technical teams that want self-hosting, source access, visual workflows, and the ability to insert code into an automation.**

n8n combines a node-based editor with code steps, API requests, credential management, and [cloud or self-hosted deployment](https://docs.n8n.io/choose-how-to-use-n8n/). Its paid plans meter production workflow executions rather than separately counting each internal step, although buyers should compare actual execution patterns rather than assuming one billing model is always cheaper.

n8n should be described as source-available rather than open source. As of August 2026, n8n’s [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/) permits many internal and consulting uses but is not an OSI-approved open-source license and restricts some commercial hosting or resale scenarios. Organizations with redistribution, white-label, or embedded-service requirements should review the license and [OEM terms](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/host-n8n/deploy-as-an-oem-integration/) directly.

For a deeper product comparison, read the [best n8n alternatives](https://www.sim.ai/library/n8n-alternatives).

## When is Activepieces a better alternative to Zapier?

**Activepieces is a better Zapier alternative for teams that want familiar no-code automation with the option to deploy the community edition on their own infrastructure.**

Activepieces focuses on trigger-and-action flows and reusable connectors, making it approachable for teams moving from conventional SaaS automation. Its deployment flexibility can be useful for organizations with data-location requirements or internal platform teams.

License wording matters here. As of August 2026, Activepieces states that its [core is open source under MIT while cloud and enterprise features use a commercial license](https://www.activepieces.com/docs/about/license). Its [pricing page](https://www.activepieces.com/pricing) describes credit-based managed plans, while the community edition is self-hosted and unmetered. Teams should confirm which edition contains each required capability before deployment.

Activepieces is a closer functional match to traditional Zapier workflows than to a full AI-agent development environment. Teams should also compare the availability and maturity of the exact connectors they require.

## When is Pipedream a better alternative to Zapier?

**Pipedream is a better Zapier alternative for developers who want managed event sources and authentication combined with [JavaScript, Python, APIs, and reusable code](https://pipedream.com/docs/workflows/building-workflows/code).**

Pipedream’s [components](https://pipedream.com/docs/components) run on its serverless infrastructure and combine reusable sources and actions with managed authentication. This reduces the infrastructure work involved in receiving events, connecting APIs, and running short code-based workflow steps.

Pipedream is less suitable for business users who want every workflow to be maintained through a purely no-code interface. Its [compute-credit model](https://pipedream.com/docs/pricing/faq) should also be tested with representative workloads before purchase.

## When is Gumloop a better alternative to Zapier?

**Gumloop is a better Zapier alternative for nontechnical teams that want hosted, visual AI workflows for research, document processing, extraction, and content operations.**

Gumloop emphasizes AI models and data-processing nodes in a no-code canvas. This can shorten the path from an AI use case to a functioning internal workflow, particularly when self-hosting and deep code extensibility are not requirements.

Gumloop is a proprietary hosted service, so teams that need software-level control, an OSI-approved license, or self-managed deployment should compare it with Sim. Its [credit documentation](https://docs.gumloop.com/core-concepts/credits) explains that consumption varies with models, tools, and runtime, so organizations should evaluate representative usage.

## When is Microsoft Power Automate a better alternative to Zapier?

**Microsoft Power Automate is a better Zapier alternative for organizations whose users, data, identity, and applications already center on Microsoft products.**

Power Automate connects cloud and desktop automation across the Microsoft ecosystem. Its [on-premises data gateway](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/gateway-manage) can connect cloud workflows to internal data sources, but this connectivity should not be confused with self-hosting the Power Automate platform.

Microsoft documents both [user and capacity licensing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/power-automate-licensing/types), including process capacity for cloud flows or machines and options for unattended desktop automation. Procurement teams should validate the intended workflow against Microsoft’s current licensing materials rather than relying on a simple per-user comparison.

## When is Workato a better alternative to Zapier?

**Workato is a better Zapier alternative for large organizations that prioritize governance, lifecycle controls, enterprise integrations, and vendor-supported deployment programs.**

Workato combines integration and automation capabilities with [security and governance controls](https://www.workato.com/platform/security) intended for cross-departmental use. It can be a strong fit when an integration center of excellence needs to govern automations created across business and technical teams.

Workato is generally a sales-led enterprise purchase rather than a lightweight self-service tool. Its current direct-customer model combines a [platform edition fee with usage fees](https://docs.workato.com/en/pricing). Smaller teams should weigh whether they need its governance and support model before entering an enterprise contract.

## How do Zapier alternatives compare on open source and self-hosting?

**Sim provides the clearest combination of an OSI-approved open-source license and self-hosting among these Zapier alternatives.**

Self-hosting and open source are separate attributes. A product can expose its source and permit self-managed deployment without using an OSI-approved license.

- **Sim** is licensed under [Apache 2.0](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/LICENSE), an [OSI-approved](https://opensource.org/licenses) open-source license, and supports self-hosting.
- **n8n** supports self-hosting but uses the [source-available Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/privacy-and-security/sustainable-use-license/), which is not OSI-approved.
- **Activepieces** supports self-managed deployment; its [core is MIT-licensed, while cloud and enterprise features are commercially licensed](https://www.activepieces.com/docs/about/license).
- **Make, Pipedream, Gumloop, Microsoft Power Automate, and Workato** document vendor-operated platforms, with agents or gateways available in some products to reach private systems rather than self-host the complete platform.

Self-hosting also transfers responsibility for infrastructure, upgrades, security patches, monitoring, backups, and model or API costs to the operator. It is not automatically less expensive than a managed service.

## How should you compare Zapier alternative pricing?

**Zapier alternatives should be compared with a representative workflow because tasks, credits, executions, compute time, and enterprise capacity are not equivalent billing units.**

A useful cost test should record:

1. The number of workflow starts per month.
2. The average number of records processed per start.
3. The number of billable steps, operations, or credits each record consumes.
4. Whether retries, polling, loops, and failed runs consume capacity.
5. AI-model charges that are billed separately from the automation platform.
6. Premium connectors, users, environments, or governance features required by the team.
7. Infrastructure and operations costs for self-hosted software.

As of August 2026, the vendors in this comparison use materially different packaging and billing systems. Exact prices and plan limits should be taken from the linked vendor pricing and licensing pages at the time of purchase.

## What is the final verdict on the best Zapier alternative?

**Sim is the best overall Zapier alternative for AI-native workflows, while [Make](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) is best for visual business automation and [n8n](https://docs.n8n.io/choose-how-to-use-n8n/) is best for technical teams seeking source access and self-hosting.**

Zapier still makes sense for teams that prioritize familiar SaaS automation and a broad catalog of ready-made actions. The right reason to replace it is not that another platform wins every category; it is that another platform better matches the team’s workflows, deployment requirements, technical skills, and cost structure.
