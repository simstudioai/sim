## TL;DR

Five Agentforce alternatives fit different enterprise operating models:

- **Sim** fits IT teams that need agents to work across Salesforce and the rest of their technology stack, with model choice and cloud or self-hosted deployment.
- **Microsoft Copilot Studio** fits organizations standardized on Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Azure, and Power Platform.
- **Workato** fits integration-led programs that prioritize governed process orchestration.
- **Relevance AI** fits business teams that want managed multi-agent systems.
- **n8n** fits technical teams that want self-hosting and code-level workflow control.

[Salesforce Agentforce](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/) remains a credible choice when Salesforce is the primary system of engagement and the team wants Salesforce-native administration, governance, and support. Teams often evaluate alternatives when they need broader cross-system orchestration, more model choice, self-hosting, or a billing model that aligns more closely with long-running back-office processes.

## What are the best Salesforce Agentforce alternatives in 2026?

The right Salesforce Agentforce alternative depends on where agents act, who operates them, and how the organization governs models, credentials, and deployment.

1. **Sim:** Salesforce agents that must act across a wider enterprise stack
2. **Microsoft Copilot Studio:** organizations standardized on Microsoft 365, Azure, and Power Platform
3. **Workato:** integration-led enterprise automation with AI steps
4. **Relevance AI:** managed multi-agent processes used by business teams
5. **n8n:** technical teams that want self-hosting and code-level flexibility

Agentforce is the baseline rather than a ranked alternative because it remains a practical option for Salesforce-centered deployments.

## When should enterprise IT choose Sim instead of Agentforce?

Sim fits when Salesforce is one important system among many rather than the center of every agent process.

Sim is the open-source AI workspace where teams build, deploy, and manage AI agents. Teams can use Salesforce through Sim's [native integration](https://docs.sim.ai/integrations/salesforce) or connect to [Salesforce Hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) Servers](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/hosted-mcp-servers/guide/hosted-mcp-servers-overview.html), which let agents discover and call tools exposed by Salesforce, then continue the same process across databases, communication tools, ticketing systems, finance applications, and internal APIs.

Sim also gives teams several model and deployment choices:

- Workspace BYOK keys work on every Sim Cloud plan, while organization-level keys are available on Pro for Teams, Max for Teams, and Enterprise, as described in the [Sim cost documentation](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/costs).
- Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, and LiteLLM work on any [self-hosted Sim deployment](https://docs.sim.ai/platform/self-hosting).
- Sim's core is licensed under Apache 2.0. Features in `apps/sim/ee`, including SSO, SCIM, access control, audit logs, data retention, and credential groups, use the separate [Sim Enterprise License](https://github.com/simstudioai/sim/blob/main/apps/sim/ee/LICENSE), which requires an active Sim Enterprise subscription for production use.

Agentforce may fit better when the agent primarily serves Salesforce teams, relies heavily on Salesforce-native data and permissions, and benefits from one Salesforce support and procurement relationship.

## When should enterprise IT choose Microsoft Copilot Studio instead of Agentforce?

Microsoft Copilot Studio fits organizations whose identity, productivity, data, and automation standards are centered on Microsoft.

Copilot Studio uses [Power Platform connectors](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/advanced-connectors) to work with Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, and other services. Its main advantage is alignment with Microsoft administration and governance rather than Salesforce depth.

Choose Copilot Studio when Microsoft environment management and existing Power Platform skills outweigh the benefit of a Salesforce-native agent layer. Choose Agentforce when Salesforce records, permissions, and user experiences dominate the use case.

## When should enterprise IT choose Workato instead of Agentforce?

Workato fits when enterprise integration architecture and governed business-process automation matter more than an agent-first building experience.

Workato's published editions include application integration, process automation, recipe lifecycle management, and separate [development, test, and production environments](https://www.workato.com/pricing). Its on-premises agent can [connect Workato Cloud to applications and databases behind a firewall](https://docs.workato.com/on-prem.html).

Choose Workato when an integration team owns the program and needs established connectivity and lifecycle controls. Choose an AI workspace when model experimentation, agent reasoning, and rapid agent composition are the primary requirements.

## When should enterprise IT choose Relevance AI instead of Agentforce?

Relevance AI fits teams that want managed multi-agent systems without operating self-hosted infrastructure.

Relevance AI documents [Workforces as teams of specialized agents](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/core-concepts/workforces) that collaborate on multi-step work. Its integrations can connect external services, run prebuilt actions, and make custom API calls.

Choose Relevance AI when a managed multi-agent experience is the priority. Choose Agentforce when Salesforce-native administration and CRM context are more important. Buyers should still compare connector actions, governance controls, and usage allowances against their production requirements.

## When should enterprise IT choose n8n instead of Agentforce?

n8n fits technical automation teams that value self-hosting, custom code, and low-level workflow control.

The [n8n Salesforce node](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/app-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.salesforce) supports common record operations and can be used as an AI tool. Teams can combine it with HTTP requests, AI nodes, and a [Code node that runs JavaScript or Python](https://docs.n8n.io/build/code-in-n8n/using-the-code-node).

[n8n Cloud pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/) is based primarily on workflow executions. Technical teams should model how loops, retries, and sub-workflows affect their expected volume.

n8n is source-available under the [Sustainable Use License](https://docs.n8n.io/n8n-community-license/community-license/license-faq), not OSI-approved open source. Organizations that require an OSI-approved core license can compare it with Sim's Apache 2.0 core while accounting for Sim's separately licensed enterprise directory.

## How should CIOs and Heads of IT choose an Agentforce alternative?

Enterprise IT leaders should test one representative Salesforce process across integration breadth, identity, failure handling, model control, deployment, governance, and total cost.

Use these questions during evaluation:

1. **Where does the process spend most of its time?** Agentforce has a natural advantage when most actions and context remain inside Salesforce.
2. **Which systems must the agent change?** Test real read and write actions across Salesforce, ERP, ticketing, messaging, databases, and internal APIs.
3. **Who controls models and credentials?** Determine whether teams can use approved model providers, BYOK, scoped secrets, and local models where required.
4. **What happens when an action fails?** Test retries, timeouts, duplicate prevention, partial completion, and human escalation.
5. **Can administrators govern multiple teams?** Compare SSO, SCIM, role controls, credential boundaries, audit logs, retention, and environment separation.
6. **What is the billing unit?** Model conversations, credits, users, workflow executions, actions, infrastructure, and model tokens against the same production workload.
7. **Can the service meet deployment policy?** Confirm whether vendor-managed cloud, regional hosting, private connectivity, or self-hosting is required.

A short proof of concept should update a Salesforce record, call at least one non-Salesforce system, pause for an approval, recover from a failed action, and produce an auditable run record. That test reveals more than a feature checklist.

## What are the key facts enterprise buyers should know at a glance?

- **Salesforce Agentforce** is a proprietary Salesforce cloud service with [consumption and per-user pricing options](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/), depending on the product.
- **Sim** offers cloud and self-hosted deployment. Sim's core is Apache 2.0, while `apps/sim/ee` uses the separate Sim Enterprise License. Sim Cloud uses subscription and usage pricing with BYOK support.
- **Microsoft Copilot Studio** is a proprietary Microsoft cloud service with [prepaid capacity and pay-as-you-go options](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/pricing/copilot-studio).
- **Workato** is a proprietary managed service with [published plan editions](https://www.workato.com/pricing) and contract-specific enterprise terms.
- **Relevance AI** is a proprietary managed cloud service with [subscription plans, actions, and vendor-credit allowances](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/pricing).
- **n8n** offers cloud and self-hosted deployment under its source-available Sustainable Use License, with [cloud pricing based on workflow executions](https://n8n.io/pricing/).
